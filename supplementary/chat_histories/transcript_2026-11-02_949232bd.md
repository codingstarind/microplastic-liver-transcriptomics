## Findings worth discussing

3. **Polyethylene is confounded with processing.** Every polyethylene library is smaller than every vehicle library, and they were prepared later in the sequence. This is a technical alternative explanation that cannot be fully removed.
4. **Several of the paper's gene changes reproduce descriptively.** Fat-synthesis genes (Fasn, Pnpla3, Acly, Me1) are lower with polyethylene, and Plin4 and Cyp4a14 are higher. Ppara itself barely changes in the bulk data.

6. **Corrections to the source records:**
   - GEO lists 4 control-diet + vehicle mice, but the paper says 5 per group.
   - "Chow Diet" in GEO is actually a purified low-fat diet.
   - Mice were caged by group, so cage effects can't be separated from treatment.
