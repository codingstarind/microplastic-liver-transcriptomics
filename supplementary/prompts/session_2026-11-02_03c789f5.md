# Slide 9 — Does PE mimic metabolic dysfunction?

### Headline

**“A seemingly strong PE–MASH similarity became more modest after correcting a hidden statistical bias.”**

This slide demonstrates our analytical depth.

Research question:

“Do the genes altered by PE move in the same direction as genes altered by the MASH-inducing diet?”

A naive comparison gave a Spearman correlation of approximately 0.54 under control diet.

However, both comparisons used the same control mice. This creates shared-reference noise that can inflate the correlation.

We split the reference mice into disjoint sets and recalculated the agreement.

Findings:

- Naive correlation: 0.54.
- Split-reference correlation under control diet: approximately 0.33.
- Split-reference correlation under MASH diet: approximately 0.05.

Use the actual figure:

https://github.com/codingstarind/microplastic-liver-transcriptomics/blob/phase2/factorial-analysis/figures/phase2/provisional_deposited/fig17_pe_vs_diet_concordance.png

There is modest exploratory resemblance under the control diet but no convincing general PE–MASH mimicry.
