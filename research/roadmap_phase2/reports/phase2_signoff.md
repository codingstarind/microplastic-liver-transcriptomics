## 2. GSE316023: rounding and length offset

**Measured on the 16 primary-contrast samples:**
- **Rounding.** 6.2% of cells are non-integer. Rounding changes at most **0.0012%** of any sample's total counts. 9,050 non-integer cells have values below 10. The fractional parts are spread over 0–0.5, so these are genuine estimated (multi-mapping-allocated) counts, not float noise.
- **Length.** The effective length implied by `count / (FPKM × N / 1e9)` has a median CV of 3% across samples for 14,176 expressed genes. However, **603 genes (4.3%) differ by more than 1.2× between GAN and chow** in median implied length, and 2,091 genes differ by more than 7%.
- **Correction:** my earlier "median CV 1%" figure was across all 77 samples and all genes. It understated the gene-specific differences between conditions.

**Assumptions required before DESeq2:**
1. The deposited values are the authors' estimated **counts** (not TPM or FPKM). This is supported by the column sums (33–49 M, close to the read numbers) and by an FPKM file that is consistent with them. The counting software is NOT STATED.
2. Rounding estimated counts to integers is acceptable; the measured impact is negligible.
3. **A per-sample, per-gene effective-length offset is needed** for genes whose dominant isoform shifts with diet. Without one, about 600 genes could show diet "effects" caused by length or isoform changes.

   **Plan:**
   - The primary fit supplies the provided `implied_length.csv.gz` as a tximport-style average-transcript-length offset (normalisation factors).
   - The fit without the offset is a sensitivity check.
   - Zero-count cells take the gene's mean implied length.
   - 16 of the 11,888 universe genes have no implied length at all (duplicated original identifiers or no usable FPKM); they fall back to no offset.
4. The FPKM denominator N equals the column total of the count file. This is consistent: the median GAN/chow log-ratio of implied length is 0.000.
5. Standard negative-binomial assumptions hold: independent mice and no batch variable within the contrast. No batch variable is deposited, which is an unverifiable limitation.

## 4. Fixed Phase 3 inputs (generated by `scripts/08_phase3_inputs.py`)

- **Muscle exclusion rule** (`metadata/muscle_exclusion_genes.csv`, **309 genes**, 187 of them in our filtered set):
  - median gastrocnemius RPKM ≥ 10 **and** ≥ 20 × (median clean-liver RPKM + 1), from GSE107787's own 36 muscle and 21 muscle-free liver samples;
  - plus 8 canonical sarcomere markers, all of which the rule also selects.
  - The source is independent tissue data, and the thresholds were not tuned against PE results.
- **Gene universe** (`metadata/phase3_gene_universe.csv`):
  - **main = 11,888 genes:** GSE294180 filtered genes, present in all four primary references, minus muscle genes;
  - **sensitivity = 8,360 genes:** additionally detected in all four primary contrasts.
  - Every method (DEG overlap, correlation, regression) uses the same universe.
- **Eligible samples** (`metadata/phase3_eligible_samples.csv`): 173 rows (contrast × sample) with GSM, matrix column, muscle score and flags.
  - `eligible_main` is true for all rows; **no sample is excluded** from the main fits.
  - `in_clean_sensitivity` defines the restricted subsets above.
  - The file is tested against `reference_contrast_plan.csv`.
