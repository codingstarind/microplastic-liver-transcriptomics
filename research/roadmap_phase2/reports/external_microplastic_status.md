## Summary for Track B planning

- **Independent laboratories with a plastic contrast and usable liver data:** GSE294180 (ours, PE), GSE245069 (PS), the Hong lab (PE/PS/PP/PB) and GSE277772 (PS, counts not available). That is **3 labs with counts, 4 if GSE277772 is re-quantified.** The Hong-lab four series count as one lab and one study.
- **Each external study has its own shared-control structure:** GSE245069 has 1 control for 3 sizes; the Hong lab has 1 control for 4 polymers. Contrasts within a study are correlated and must not be treated as independent replications.
- **The technical-depth check (Phase 1 gap G4) is cheap.** Run tables are already in `metadata/run_metadata.csv` and can be done in Phase 5 without new downloads.
