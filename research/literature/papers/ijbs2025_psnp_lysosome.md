## Access level read
Full text (Europe PMC XML -> PMC12210384.txt), including Methods, all main figure legends, Discussion, Acknowledgements and data statement. ## Biological question
Whether oral polystyrene nanoplastics (PS-NPs) aggravate HFD-induced MASLD, and the cellular mechanism: "we aimed to explore the combined effects of obesity and NPs on human health by the concurrent administration of a HFD and PS-NPs to mice and human liver cell lines" (Introduction, final paragraph). Mechanistic hypothesis: PS-NP endocytosis -> lysosomal accumulation -> impaired acidification/cathepsin maturation -> blocked autophagic flux -> lipid accumulation.

## Experimental design
IN VIVO 2x2 factorial, two durations (8 and 20 weeks): "i) control diet (CD, n=6 ...); ii) control diet with PS-NPs (0.5 mg) per day (CD + PS-NPs, n=6); iii) HFD (n=6) with 45 kcal% fat (D12451; Research Diet...), or iv) HFD with PS-NPs (HFD + PS-NPs, n=6) for 8 or 20 weeks" (Methods, Animal studies). Randomisation stated: "Mice were randomly assigned to four groups" (Results, first section; also Fig. 1A legend). Vehicle control = control jelly cube without PS-NPs (well-matched vehicle: same agarose cube). Individually housed: "To ensure that the PS-NPs were dosed accurately, mice were individually housed and weighed before and after the study" (Methods) - so cage = mouse, which removes cage confounding. Blinding: not reported (checked Methods and Statistical analysis).
IN VITRO: HepaRG (main model) plus HepG2, Hep-G2/2.2.15, Hep3B, Huh7, SK-HEP-1, PLC/PRF/5 and 293T screened for vacuolisation/lipid accumulation (Fig. 3); PS-NP dose-response 0/10/20/50/100 ug/mL, 24 or 48 h; oleate (0.2 mM, 3 h pre-treatment) co-exposure; endocytosis inhibitors (3-MA, cytochalasin B, 5-(N-ethyl-N-isopropyl)amiloride, chlorpromazine, genistein, wortmannin); autophagy modulators rapamycin (1-2 ug/mL) and bafilomycin A1 (10 nM).

## Species/strain/sex/age; tissue(s)
"PS-NPs were administered to C57BL/6J male mice (DBL, Eumseoung-gun ... Republic of Korea)" (Methods, Animal studies). Males only; no female arm. Age: "Following a 2-week acclimation period, 4-week-old mice were grouped as follows" (Methods) - i.e. exposure began at 4 weeks of age (note: the 2-week acclimation and the stated 4-week age are not explicitly reconciled in the text; worth checking). Tissues: liver (histology, Oil Red O, Picro-Sirius Red, immunofluorescence for F4/80 macrophages, MPO/neutrophils, RNA-seq, qPCR), serum (ALT, AST, cholesterol, TG), body and perfused liver weights. Human cell line HepaRG for mechanism. Ethics: KRIBB IACUC, approval number KRIBB-AEC-21279.

## Biological replicates per group
n=6 per group per diet arm, stated both in Methods and Fig. 1A legend ("CD, n=6", "CD+PS-NPs, n=6", "HFD, n=6", "HFD+PS-NPs, n=6"), for each of the 8- and 20-week experiments (total mice per duration = 24; whether 8- and 20-week cohorts are separate animals is implied but not stated explicitly).
RNA-seq n per group: NOT REPORTED. The transcriptome section and Fig. 2 legend never state how many livers were sequenced ("Each column represents a sample" in the Fig. 2B heatmap legend, without a count). This is a gap for anyone judging the DEG counts. Searched Methods ("Total RNA extraction, real-time PCR, and transcriptome analysis"), Results and Fig. 2 legend.
Histology sampling: "Three spots from three lobes in each tissue section were randomly chosen to assess the relative area of fatty changes" (Methods, Histochemical evaluation). In vitro replicate numbers per experiment: not reported in the legends read.

## Polymer; particle size; supplier; label; characterisation; endotoxin
Verbatim (Methods, Chemicals): "Carboxyl-modified polystyrenes (PS-COOH NPs) with a size of 50 nm (2.5% w/v aqueous suspensions including 3.64 x 10^14 particles/mL) were purchased from Polysciences, Inc. (Warrington, PA, USA)." Polymer = polystyrene, carboxyl surface modification (anionic); nominal size 50 nm; supplier Polysciences (catalogue number not given).
MEASURED size/zeta/DLS: not reported (no DLS, zeta potential, TEM sizing, or dispersion-stability data in Methods or Results). Fluorescent label: a GFP/Yellow-Green conjugated PS-NP was used for imaging - "Visualization of PS-NP accumulation in the acidic compartment using GFP-conjugated PS-NPs (PS-NP-GFP)" (Fig. 6C legend) and "Experiments using Yellow Green-conjugated PS-NPs" (Results) - but its supplier/size is not separately specified.
ENDOTOXIN: not reported (searched Methods and Results). Note the Introduction cites others' work on "elevated levels of plasma lipopolysaccharide" with dietary NPs, but no endotoxin assay of the particles themselves is described here.
Size rationale given in Discussion: "30 to 80nm size range (50 nm for this study) could imitate ingested particulates as it is comparable size to chylomicron remnants".

## Dose; route; vehicle; frequency; duration
Route: oral, via a medicated jelly cube (voluntary consumption, not gavage). "Oral administration involved the use of PS-NP-jelly cubes prepared as previously described 28, containing either 0 or 0.5 mg PS-NPs with 2.5% agarose" (Methods). Dose: 0.5 mg PS-NPs per mouse per day ("control diet with PS-NPs (0.5 mg) per day"), i.e. an absolute per-animal dose, NOT normalised to body weight; the paper does not state a mg/kg figure and does not report whether cubes were fully consumed. Vehicle: identical 2.5% agarose cube with 0 mg PS-NPs. Frequency: daily. Duration: 8 or 20 weeks, concurrent with the diet.
In vitro: 0, 10, 20, 50, 100 ug/mL for 24-48 h; cytotoxicity low - "the IC50 value exceeded 1,000 ug/mL (Fig. S3)".

## Diet / metabolic-stress model
Control diet: "Teklad Global 18% protein rodent diet; Teklad 2018S; Envigo" (grain-based chow, NOT a purified low-fat control). HFD: "45 kcal% fat (D12451; Research Diet, New Brunswick, NJ, USA)". This is a classical obesogenic HFD model (body-weight gain observed), unlike the CDAHFD model in liverint2025_np_masld. CD and HFD are not ingredient-matched (chow vs purified HFD).

## Data types
- Bulk liver RNA-seq: "mRNA was sequenced by ebiogen Inc. (Seoul, Republic of Korea). Detailed information on the methods is provided in Table S4." Platform, read length, depth and aligner are NOT in the main text (deferred to Table S4, not read) - NOT VERIFIED.
- qRT-PCR (SYBR Green, 2^-ddCT, reference gene RPL13A), Western blot (beta-actin loading control), immunofluorescence/confocal, flow cytometry (BODIPY), H&E, Oil Red O, Picro-Sirius Red, serum chemistry (ALT/AST/CHO/TG), cathepsin B fluorometric activity assay, CYP3A4 P450-Glo activity, intracellular TG assay, LysoTracker/LysoSensor/MitoTracker/ER-Tracker/dextran imaging, crystal violet vacuole staining.

## Statistics & computational methods
Verbatim (Methods, Statistical analysis): "Statistical significance was assessed using Tukey's multiple comparison tests or Student's t-tests following one-way analysis of variance using GraphPad Prism 9 software ... The error bars represent the standard error of the mean. Asterisks denote p values less than 0.05, 0.01, 0.001, or 0.0001."
DE thresholds (Results; Fig. 2 legend): "DEGs were selected under the following conditions: P value < 0.05, |log2 fold change| > 0.5". NOTE: a RAW (unadjusted) p-value threshold at the DEG step - no FDR correction there, and no DE tool (DESeq2/edgeR/limma) named in the main text. Correction is applied only downstream, to enrichment: "over-representation analysis with the ClusterProfiler 4.0 package in R, using parameters set as adjusted p < 0.05 (Benjamini-Hochberg method)", with GO and KEGG.
INTERACTION TESTING: NO formal diet x PS-NP interaction term anywhere. In vivo phenotypes use one-way ANOVA + Tukey against a single reference: "Comparisons are with the CD group unless otherwise noted" (Fig. 1E,F legend). The transcriptome is analysed as THREE SEPARATE PAIRWISE CONTRASTS (CD+PS-NPs vs CD; HFD+PS-NPs vs HFD; HFD+PS-NPs vs CD+PS-NPs) plus a Venn overlap - no interaction model, no two-factor design matrix, and no HFD vs CD contrast reported in Fig. 2C. The words "synergistically"/"synergistic" appear (Abstract; Results) with no formal synergy or interaction statistic behind them.

## Main findings with effect directions and statistical evidence
IN VIVO (20 weeks unless stated)
- Body/liver weight: "the HFD group demonstrated a 25% increase in body weight and a 20% increase in liver weight compared with the CD group, whereas the HFD+PS-NPs group displayed a 37% increase in body weight and a substantial 61% increase in liver weight compared with the CD+PS-NPs group" (Results 1; Fig. 1B). VALIDATED (weights). Direction UP. NOTE the two percentages use DIFFERENT reference groups (CD vs CD+PS-NPs), so they are not a like-for-like HFD vs HFD+PS-NPs comparison.
- Duration dependence: at 8 weeks "only body weight significantly increased (Fig. S1A)" and "8-week of oral PS-NPs administration was not enough to affect the levels of ALT, AST, and CHO (Fig. S1G)". VALIDATED.
- Serum: "the mice fed both HFD and PS-NPs exhibited significantly increased serum ALT, AST, and CHO levels than those in CD, CD+PS-NPs, and HFD alone (Fig. 1E), but no notable differences were observed in serum triglyceride (TG) levels between each group (Fig. 1F)". VALIDATED. Directions: ALT UP, AST UP, cholesterol UP, serum TG unchanged. This is the clearest HFD+PS-NPs > HFD contrast in the paper, and it CONTRASTS with liverint2025_np_masld where NPs did not change ALT/AST.
- Steatosis grade: "CD and CD+PS-NPs mice displayed no dramatic changes in fat accumulation"; "20 weeks of HFD exhibited zonal aggregates of mainly microvesicular hepatocytes, whereas HFD+PS-NPs group had diffuse lesions containing a mixture of macrovesicular and microvesicular hepatocytes (Fig. S1B)". VALIDATED (H&E, Oil Red O quantified in ImageJ; Fig. 1C,D). Direction UP.
- Inflammation: "oral administration of PS-NPs induced hepatic inflammation in CD-fed mice as well as HFD-fed mice, as evidenced by increased macrophages (Fig. S1C and S1D) and neutrophils (Fig. S1E and S1F)" - one PS-NP effect that IS present on the control diet, qualifying the "no effects on chow" headline. VALIDATED (immunofluorescence F4/80, MPO).
- Fibrosis - INTERNALLY INCONSISTENT: histology says "The exposure to PS-NPs showed increased collagen deposition in CD or HFD fed mice compared to those in the CD-alone and HFD-alone groups (Fig. S1H and S1I)" (VALIDATED, Picro-Sirius Red), whereas the transcriptome says "fibrosis-related genes were not significantly altered (Fig. S2E), suggesting that the concentration and duration of HFD exposure applied in this study were insufficient to fully induce liver fibrosis" (OMICS-ONLY). The paper does not reconcile the two.
- Transcriptome (OMICS-ONLY): "361 DEGs in the CD+PS-NPs versus the CD group; 628 DEGs in the HFD+PS-NPs versus the HFD group; and 1501 DEGs in the HFD+PS-NPs versus CD+PS-NPs group"; splits 169 up/192 down (CD+PS-NPs vs CD), 226 up/402 down (HFD+PS-NPs vs HFD), 775 up/726 down (HFD+PS-NPs vs CD+PS-NPs); union 2180 DEGs; "The two comparative pairs ... shared 16 DEGs, including 11 coding proteins related to immune regulation and actin organization, such as Ctse, Il7r, Cd46, Mt1, Ophn1, and Myom3" (Results 2; Fig. 2C,D). The plastic effect is modest (361 and 628 genes at uncorrected p<0.05) next to the diet effect.
- Enriched themes (OMICS-ONLY): "PPAR signaling pathway, lipid metabolism (biosynthetic and catabolic processes), cell-cell adhesion, and actin organization" (Fig. 2E,F); Discussion adds "PPAR signaling, AMPK signaling, and fatty acid biosynthesis pathways". The up/down direction of the PPAR pathway is conveyed only by bar colour in Fig. 2F and is not stated in words - NOT VERIFIED from text.
- Myc: "MYC ... was significantly increased in mouse liver tissue obtained from HFD+PS-NPs group (Fig. S2 and 4D)" (liver qPCR, VALIDATED); dose-dependently increased in HepaRG and "further pronounced following OA treatment (Fig. 4E and 4F)" (VALIDATED, qPCR + Western).
IN VITRO (HepaRG)
- Cell-type specificity: "The formation of large vacuoles and a substantial increase in lipid accumulation were only observed in HepaRG cells (Fig. 3)" among HepaRG/HepG2/SK-HEP-1/293T. VALIDATED (crystal violet, BODIPY). Important caveat for generalising the mechanism.
- Lipid accumulation UP dose-dependently (BODIPY, Nile Red, flow cytometry; Fig. 5A-C); intracellular TG "modestly increased" (Fig. 5D). VALIDATED.
- Oleate co-exposure: "Concurrent treatment with PS-NPs and oleate resulted in greater lipid accumulation compared to treatment with PS-NPs alone, consistent with the observations from our in vivo studies (Fig. 5H and 5I)" - the in vitro analogue of the diet interaction, again pairwise. "However, oleate was not associated with the PS-NP-induced lysosomal changes (Fig. 5H-5J)" - the lysosomal phenotype is lipid-independent.
- Lipogenesis/transport genes UP: "upregulation of genes involved in lipogenesis, including peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor gamma (PPARG). Additionally, CD36, perilipin 2 (PLIN2), and PPARG coactivator 1a (PGC-1A) and upregulated"; C/EBPA UP (mRNA + protein); FABP1 DOWN; C/EBPB unchanged; HO-1 UP and IL-6 UP (Fig. 5L-N). VALIDATED (qPCR + Western blot).
- Hepatocyte identity/function: HNF4A DOWN, ALB DOWN, CYP3A4 UP (mRNA, protein and activity), dose-dependent (Fig. 5E-G). VALIDATED.
- Cathepsins (titular mechanism): "The protein levels of CtsD were proportionally increased, but predominantly existed in immature forms, pro-CtsD and single chain CtsD, along with a concurrent decrease in the levels of mature form of CtsD (Fig. 4B). Additionally, the activity of the lysosomal enzyme CtsB was also reduced by more than 80% (Fig. 4C)." VALIDATED (Western blot + enzymatic activity).
- Uptake route: "Each inhibitor reduced the uptake of 50 nm PS-NPs by more than 50%. Inhibition of actin-, caveolin-, and clathrin-mediated endocytosis significantly decreased the internalization ... by more than 80%"; clathrin inhibitor CPZ most effective. VALIDATED (pharmacological inhibition). Minor internal inconsistency: Results call macropinocytosis minor and clathrin predominant, while one Discussion sentence says "clathrin- and caveolin-mediated endocytosis".
- Vacuole identity: EEA1-negative, RAB7/LAMP1/LAMP2/LGP85-positive, dextran-filled, acidic by LysoSensor (Fig. 6D-G). VALIDATED (confocal colocalisation).
- Autophagy block: p62 and LC3-II accumulate dose- and time-dependently; mTORC1 activity UP (p-S6K1 UP); TFEB translocates to the nucleus; rapamycin reduces vacuoles, p-S6K1, PLIN2, p62/LC3 and lipid; BafA1 raises p62 and lipid, and "the decrease in the difference in the p62 ratio based on Baf A1 treatment in the presence of PS-NPs indicate that PS-NP treatment impairs autophagic flux (Fig. 7J and 7K)". VALIDATED by pharmacological rescue and a flux assay - in vitro only; there is no in vivo rapamycin, knockdown, or cathepsin-restoration experiment.
NOTE on the title: the title says "Impaired Lysosomal Acidification", but the imaging shows the large vacuoles ARE acidic ("demonstrated the acidic pH of these large vacuoles", Fig. 6E) and LysoTracker signal INCREASES. The acidification claim rests on the BafA1 analogy and on blocked cathepsin maturation, not on a direct lysosomal pH measurement showing de-acidification - worth checking against Fig. 6E.

## Pathway-theme directions
- Lipid metabolism / lipogenesis: UP. In vitro PPARG, CD36, PLIN2, PGC-1A, C/EBPA all UP; FABP1 DOWN (Fig. 5L-N, VALIDATED). In vivo: KEGG "PPAR signaling pathway", "lipid metabolism (biosynthetic and catabolic processes)" and "fatty acid biosynthesis" enriched (OMICS-ONLY). Fasn, Srebf1, Acly, Scd1 are not individually reported in the main text.
- PPAR / FAO: PPAR signalling is the most-cited enriched KEGG pathway, but it is PPARgamma (lipid storage) that is measured and UP; PPARalpha antibody is listed in Methods yet no PPARalpha result is reported in the main text. Ppara, Cpt1a, Acox1 and Cyp4a are not reported. AMPK signalling enriched (Discussion, Fig. 2F) - direction not stated.
- Cholesterol: serum cholesterol UP in HFD+PS-NPs (Fig. 1E, VALIDATED). Hepatic cholesterol-synthesis genes not reported.
- Inflammation: UP. Hepatic macrophages (F4/80) and neutrophils (MPO) increased by PS-NPs on BOTH diets (Fig. S1C-S1F, VALIDATED); "genes involved in inflammatory responses were significantly enriched following PS-NPs treatment (Fig. S2C and S2D)" (OMICS-ONLY); IL-6 mRNA UP in HepaRG (VALIDATED). Tnf, Il1b, NF-kB and NLRP3 are not individually reported.
- Oxidative stress: HO-1 (HMOX1) UP in HepaRG (Fig. 5L, VALIDATED) - described as one of "two key oxidative regulators". No ROS, MDA, SOD, GSH or Nrf2 measurement in vivo; this paper is far weaker than liverint2025_np_masld on oxidative stress.
- Mitochondria: only imaging (MitoTracker colocalisation of some lipid droplets, Fig. 5K); PGC-1A mRNA UP. No respirometry, no mtDNA, no OXPHOS protein - mitochondrial function essentially not assessed.
- Fibrosis: conflicting (see above) - collagen deposition UP by Picro-Sirius Red (VALIDATED) but fibrosis genes not significantly altered (OMICS-ONLY).
- Circadian: not reported (no clock gene mentioned anywhere in the main text).
- Autophagy/lysosome (the paper's core): autophagic flux DOWN, p62 UP, LC3-II UP, LAMP1/LAMP2/LGP85 UP, mature cathepsin D DOWN, cathepsin B activity DOWN >80%, mTORC1 activity UP, TFEB nuclear translocation UP. VALIDATED.
- MP x diet interaction: CLAIMED ("synergistically exacerbated", Abstract; "HFD exerts a synergistic effect with PS-NPs-induced inflammation", Results) but NOT formally tested as an interaction - one-way ANOVA + Tukey and three separate pairwise DEG contrasts. The claim "no effects were observed with PS-NPs on a normal chow diet" (Abstract) is contradicted in the paper's own Results by the CD+PS-NPs increases in macrophages, neutrophils and collagen, and by 361 DEGs in CD+PS-NPs vs CD.

## Author-acknowledged limitations
- Fibrosis under-induction: "fibrosis-related genes were not significantly altered (Fig. S2E), suggesting that the concentration and duration of HFD exposure applied in this study were insufficient to fully induce liver fibrosis" (Results, PS-NPs induces substantial changes... section).
- Controversial state of the field / mechanism incomplete: "However, research on the health risks posed by PS-NPs is still in the early stages, and further investigation is imperative to elucidate the mechanisms by which PS-NPs affect various cellular processes. These deficiencies highlight the need for additional research to unravel the underlying molecular pathways." (Discussion, final paragraph).
- Human relevance of pure particles uncertain: "some experiments involving pure MPs and NPs did not reveal significant cytotoxic effects on human cells. Therefore, the potential human health risks associated with exposure to pure MP/NPs remain controversial." (Discussion, para 1).
- Diet-dependent direction acknowledged: "Opposing effects of NPs on liver lipid metabolism in HFD- and chow-fed mice may be due to variations in basal metabolic status" (Discussion, para 3), citing a study where "oral exposure to NPs (500 nm at 1,000 ug/L) for 5 weeks in mice fed a regular diet reduced liver lipid levels".
There is no dedicated "Limitations" section; the above are the only limitation-type statements.

## Methodological points to verify independently
1. RNA-seq n per group is never stated. How many livers per group were sequenced, and were all four groups sequenced in one batch, at one time point (20 weeks) and at one time of day?
2. DEG threshold is an UNCORRECTED p<0.05 with |log2FC|>0.5 and no named DE tool. With n possibly ~3/group, how many of the 361/628 DEGs survive FDR? Is the 16-gene overlap more than chance?
3. Why is there no HFD vs CD DEG contrast in Fig. 2C? Without it one cannot judge whether the PS-NP effect is small or large relative to the diet effect, nor construct an interaction contrast.
4. Dose is per animal (0.5 mg/day), not per kg; HFD mice end 25-37% heavier, so the mg/kg exposure DIVERGES between diet arms over 20 weeks. Was consumption of each jelly cube verified? This is a possible confound for a "diet x particle" claim.
5. Diets are not ingredient-matched (Teklad 2018S chow vs D12451 purified 45% kcal fat).
6. Particle characterisation: no DLS/zeta/TEM, no check of aggregation in the jelly cube or in culture medium, no endotoxin assay, no surfactant/preservative control for the Polysciences suspension, and the GFP/YG-conjugated particles used for imaging are not characterised separately.
7. Blinding of histology and of Oil Red O / Picro-Sirius Red quantification is not reported; "three spots from three lobes ... randomly chosen" - by whom, and blinded?
8. Sex: males only, exposure starting at 4 weeks of age (pre-pubertal). Also check the stated "2-week acclimation ... 4-week-old mice" - at what age did dosing actually start?
9. Statistics: "Tukey's multiple comparison tests or Student's t-tests following one-way ANOVA" - which test was used for which panel? Fig. 5D legend says "by ANOVA two-tailed unpaired t-test", which is ambiguous.
10. The fibrosis histology vs fibrosis transcriptome discrepancy should be checked directly in Fig. S1H-I and S2E.
11. The "impaired lysosomal acidification" claim vs the LysoSensor evidence that the vacuoles are acidic (Fig. 6E) - was lysosomal pH ever quantified (ratiometric), or only imaged?
12. In vitro mechanism rests on HepaRG only, with the paper itself showing the phenotype is absent in HepG2, SK-HEP-1 and 293T; concentrations (50-100 ug/mL) are far above any plausible in vivo hepatic concentration.
13. No measurement of actual hepatic PS-NP burden in the mice (no biodistribution, no particle quantification) - the in vivo and in vitro arms are linked by inference only.

## Relevance to the team
The closest design match to the team's GSE294180 re-analysis: a 2x2 plastic x HFD mouse study WITH bulk liver RNA-seq, male C57BL/6J, n=6/group. It is directly instructive on two of the team's concerns. (i) Inference: the "synergy" claim is built from three separate pairwise DEG contrasts and a one-way ANOVA, never an interaction term - and the paper omits the HFD vs CD contrast entirely, so the relative size of diet and plastic effects cannot be judged. (ii) Thresholds: DEGs were called at uncorrected p<0.05, so DEG counts (361/628/1501) are not FDR-controlled and are not comparable to FDR-based counts. Usable directional claims for a plastic-plus-HFD liver signature: PPAR signalling/lipid metabolism and actin-organisation/cell-adhesion themes enriched; Myc UP; CD36, PLIN2, PPARG, C/EBPA UP and FABP1 DOWN (in human cells); inflammation UP on both diets; fibrosis genes unchanged. Note also that this paper has no data accession for its RNA-seq, so its DEG lists cannot be re-derived.
