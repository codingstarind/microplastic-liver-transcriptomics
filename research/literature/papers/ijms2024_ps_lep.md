## Biological question
Whether oral 500 nm polystyrene MPs for 9 weeks disrupt hepatic lipid, glucose (Glu) and amino-acid metabolism in normal (WT) versus genetically obese leptin-knockout (Lep KO) C57BL/6 mice (Introduction last paragraph; Abstract).

## Experimental design (groups, controls, randomisation/blinding if stated)
- 2 x 2 layout (genotype x MP): "vehicle-treated WT mice (n = 6), MP-treated WT mice (n = 6), vehicle-treated Lep KO mice (n = 6), and MP-treated Lep KO mice (n = 6)" (Methods 4.3). Vehicle = "1 x phosphate-buffered saline (PBS)" same volume (4.3).
- WT and Lep KO were littermate-derived offspring of heterozygous (HT) x HT matings: "8-week-old male and female HT mice were mated at a ratio of 1:2", genotyped by tail PCR (Methods 4.2). Whether WT and KO in the experiment were littermates/cage-mates: not stated.
- Randomisation, blinding (including NAFLD histology scoring), cage allocation: not reported (Methods 4.3, 4.6).
- Obesity is GENETIC (leptin deficiency), not dietary - all mice on standard chow (4.3). No high-fat/MASH diet arm.
- In vitro verification arm: HepG2 and MDI-differentiated 3T3-L1 cells, vehicle vs LoMPs vs HiMPs for 24 h (Methods 4.13).

## Species/strain/sex/age; tissue(s)
- Mouse, C57BL/6Korl (WT) and C57BL/6-Lep(em1hwl)/Korl (Lep KO); HT founders "of the C57BL/6J strain" from NIFDS, Korea (Methods 4.2).
- Age: "Seven-week-old WT (n = 12) and Lep KO mice (n = 12)" at start (4.3); ~16 weeks at end (9 weeks).
- SEX OF EXPERIMENTAL MICE: NOT REPORTED (only breeders are described as "male and female"; grep for male/female/sex in Methods finds nothing else).
- Tissues: liver, adipose (epididymal and retroperitoneal fat for weights, 4.4), serum. Cells: HepG2, 3T3-L1.

## Biological replicates per group (per assay - RNA-seq n may differ from phenotype n)
- Allocated: n = 6 per group (Methods 4.3).
- Figure legends repeatedly state "five to six mice per group", each measured "twice" (technical duplicate), e.g. Fig. 4 legend "performed on five to six mice per group and the transcription levels of each gene were analyzed twice". Applies to body/liver weight (Fig. 2), liver histology/NAFLD score (Fig. 3), liver qPCR (Figs 4, 10-12), liver western (Figs 5, 11, 12), adipose (Figs 6-8), leptin/food/water (Fig. 9).
- Serum lipids (Table 1): n not stated. Reader arithmetic (to verify, not stated by authors): reported values look like very small n, e.g. WT-MP "TG 95 +/- 19.80", "LDL 8.5 +/- 0.71", Lep KO-MP "TC 142.5 +/- 9.20" (half-integer means with SDs consistent with n = 2), Lep KO-vehicle "133.67 +/- 3.79" (consistent with n = 3), WT-vehicle "73.25" (consistent with n = 4).
- Liver metabolomics (Figs 13-14): n not stated.
- No RNA-seq in this study.
- Cells: "three to five wells per group" (Figs 15-16 legends).

## Polymer; particle size (nominal and measured); supplier; surface/fluorescent label; characterisation; endotoxin
- Polystyrene; "mean diameter size of 0.4-0.6 um and a density of 1.04-1.06 g/cm3 at a concentration of 25 mg/mL, in aqueous suspension (Sigma-Aldrich Co.)" (Methods 4.1). Catalogue number not given.
- Measured: "circular-shaped particles with a specific number-distribution size (607.23 +/- 8.27 d.nm) and zeta potential (-35.52 +/- 1.18 mV) (Figure 17)" (4.1); Fig. 17 legend says properties "analyzed as described in previous studies [50]" - unclear whether measured on this batch.
- Fluorescent: red fluorescence detected in liver sections and HepG2 cells (Fig. 1 legend; Fig. 15A legend) - so particles are fluorescent-labelled; dye/surface functionalisation not stated.
- Endotoxin: not reported.
- Tissue accumulation: qualitative fluorescence microscopy only - "MP particles with an average size of 0.5 um were detected in the liver section" (Results 2.1; Fig. 1). No quantification; no non-fluorescent-tissue autofluorescence control described.

## Dose (as reported, and units); route; vehicle; frequency; duration
- "orally administered the dispersed MPs suspension at a concentration of 100 ug/mL, once daily (0.5 mL/day) three times a week for 9 weeks" (Methods 4.3) - i.e. ~50 ug per administration (reader calculation). Route "orally administered" (gavage not explicitly named). Vehicle PBS.
- Dose rationale cites earlier constipation study (10, 50, 100 ug/L) - note unit mismatch ug/L vs ug/mL in the same sentence (4.3) - verify.
- Euthanasia "At 24 h after the last administration", CO2 (4.3).
- In vitro: HepG2 0.00125 or 0.0025 wt%; 3T3-L1 0.005 or 0.01 wt%; 24 h (4.13).

## Diet / metabolic-stress model (exact diet, % kcal fat, supplier, control diet)
- No diet manipulation. "All the mice were provided with a standard diet (Samtako Bio Korea Co., Osan, Republic of Korea) comprising nitrogen-free extracts (50.5%), crude protein (22.5%), moisture (11.0%), fat (5.0%), fiber (4.5%), and ash (6.5%)" (Methods 4.3). Fat given as % composition (not % kcal).
- Metabolic stress = genetic leptin deficiency (Lep KO; body weight "higher by 272.4% and 273.6%" than WT, Results 2.2).
- Authors explicitly note they lack "a direct comparison between the diet-induced obesity model and the gene-deficient-induced obesity model" (Conclusions).

NMR acquisition not described - verify.
- Fluorescence microscopy of particles in liver and cells.

## Statistics & computational methods (DE tool, thresholds, multiple testing, pathway tool, models; tests for phenotypes)
- "Unpaired Two-Sample t-test and the One-way Analysis of Variance (ANOVA), followed by a Tukey multiple comparisons test after assessing normality using the Shapiro-Wilk test in SPSS 27.0"; MetaboAnalyst 4.0 for metabolites; mean +/- SD; p < 0.05 (Methods 4.14).
- Legends mark "* indicates p < 0.05 compared to the vehicle-treated group. # indicates p < 0.05 compared to the WT mice" - i.e. pairwise contrasts only.
- MP x GENOTYPE INTERACTION: NOT formally tested. No two-way ANOVA or interaction term is mentioned anywhere; genotype-dependent ("opposite") MP effects are inferred from separate pairwise comparisons within each genotype.
- Which test (t-test vs one-way ANOVA/Tukey) was used for which figure: not specified.
- Metabolomics: PLS-DA/VIP selection; multiple-testing correction for the "seven metabolites" with "significant change": not reported.
- Technical duplicates ("analyzed twice") - whether averaged per mouse before testing: not stated.

## Main findings with effect directions and statistical evidence
(All pairwise MP vs vehicle within genotype; % changes as stated in Results; significance only as text/legends state.)
- NULL body weight: "no difference in the body weight of the WT and Lep KO mice between the vehicle-treated and MP-treated groups" (2.2, Fig. 2A). NULL liver weight: "no significant alteration in the weight and gross morphology of the liver" (2.2, Fig. 2C). VALIDATED (weighing).
- Liver histology - CONTRASTING by genotype: Lep KO + MP: lipid droplets and steatosis areas "significantly decreased by 26.0-49.8% ... without any difference in their ... NAFLD scores"; WT + MP: "increase in the NAFLD score and steatosis area (604.6%)" (2.3, Fig. 3A,B). VALIDATED (H&E scoring; blinding not stated).
- Liver PPARg / C/EBPa mRNA - CONTRASTING: WT + MP "increased by 74.0% and 72.0%"; Lep KO + MP "decreased by 22.7% and 15.6%" (2.3, Fig. 4A,B). Significance of the Lep KO decreases not stated in text. VALIDATED (qPCR).
- Liver FAS mRNA UP in both: "increased by 62.0% and 45.8%" (WT, Lep KO); aP2 DOWN in both: "decreased by 25.0% and 23.8%" (2.3, Fig. 4C,D). VALIDATED (qPCR).
- Liver lipolysis proteins DOWN in both: ATGL, p-HSL, p-perilipin "decreased by 19.0-89.0% and 28.3-51.5%"; "decrease rates were higher in the WT mice" (2.3, Fig. 5). VALIDATED (western).
- Serum lipids (Table 1): WT: TC 73.25 -> 90 (*), TG 50.75 -> 95 (*), LDL 4 -> 8.5 (*), HDL ns ("increased by 22.9%, 87.2%, and 11.9%" per 2.4 text; note text LDL % does not match table values - verify). Lep KO: "MP administration did not induce any significant alterations" (NULL). VALIDATED (clinical chemistry), small/unstated n.
- NULL adipose weight and adipocyte area (Fig. 6). Adipose PPARg, C/EBPa, aP2, FAS mRNA DOWN "by 33.0-51.0% and 33.3-75.0%" in both genotypes (Fig. 7); adipose ATGL, p-HSL, p-perilipin UP "16.0-9045.0% and 109.3-443.7%" (Fig. 8). VALIDATED (qPCR/western).
- Leptin UP in WT + MP: adipose "147.0%", serum "22.0%" (Fig. 9A,B); not detectable in Lep KO. NULL food intake and water consumption (Fig. 9C,D). VALIDATED (ELISA).
- Glu: serum DOWN "22.9% and 43.3%", liver UP "42.4% and 28.7%" in MP groups "regardless of the mouse type"; glycogenolysis genes (PYGL, G6PC3, phosphoglucomutase) UP "30.0-58.0% and 36.2-92.6%" (2.5, Fig. 10). VALIDATED (biochem/qPCR).
- Liver GLUT4 protein/mRNA DOWN "58.0-74.0% and 64.1-68.2%"; p-AMPK DOWN "20.0% and 14.5%" (Fig. 11). Insulin receptor protein/mRNA UP "13.0-43.0% and 22.7-39.3%"; p-IRS-1, p-PI3K, p-Akt UP, "range of their increase rates was very wide" (Fig. 12). VALIDATED (western/qPCR). Note: authors interpret increased IR/IRS-1/PI3K/Akt phosphorylation as "increase in the insulin resistance" (2.5) - interpretive.
- Liver metabolites: PLS-DA separation vehicle vs MP in both genotypes; 7 metabolites (aspartate, lysine, SAH, taurine, GSH, arginine, serine) "showed a significant change in levels in both mouse types"; mapped to "phenylalanine, tyrosine, and tryptophan biosynthesis" (2.6, Figs 13-14). Directions per metabolite not given in text (only arginine "decreases" in Discussion). OMICS-ONLY / COMPUTATIONAL (pathway mapping).
- In vitro: HepG2 PPARg mRNA UP "156.0% and 555.0%", aP2 inverse; glycogenolysis genes UP; 3T3-L1 fat accumulation DOWN, PPARg/aP2 DOWN, p-HSL/p-perilipin UP only at HiMP (2.7, Figs 15-16). VALIDATED in vitro (qPCR/western/staining).

## Pathway-theme directions (lipid, PPAR/FAO, cholesterol, inflammation, oxidative stress, mitochondria, fibrosis, circadian, interaction)
- Lipid/lipogenesis (liver): FAS UP (both genotypes); aP2 DOWN (both); PPARg/C/EBPa UP in WT, DOWN in Lep KO; steatosis UP in WT, DOWN in Lep KO; lipolysis proteins DOWN (both). Srebf1, Acly, Cd36: not measured.
- Lipid (adipose): adipogenic/lipogenic genes DOWN, lipolysis proteins UP (both genotypes).
- PPARa/FAO: not measured (Ppara, Cpt1a, Acox1, Cyp4a absent); FAO only mentioned speculatively in Discussion ("fatty acid oxidation controlled by PPARa could be considered the main mechanism ... although further research is needed").
- Cholesterol: serum TC and LDL UP in WT only; hepatic cholesterol and synthesis genes not measured.
- Inflammation: NAFLD score includes inflammation (0-4) but component scores not reported; no cytokine/macrophage markers. Authors: did not analyse "the mechanism of action of MPs on inflammation" (Conclusions).
- Oxidative stress: hepatic GSH among 7 significantly changed metabolites (direction not stated in text); taurine, SAH also changed. No ROS/MDA/SOD/Nrf2/Hmox1.
- Glucose/insulin signalling: hepatic Glu UP, serum Glu DOWN, glycogenolysis genes UP, GLUT4 and p-AMPK DOWN, IR/IRS-1/PI3K/Akt UP.
- Mitochondria: not measured (only p-AMPK). Fibrosis: not measured. Circadian: not measured; light cycle 08:00-20:00 stated (4.3); time of sacrifice not reported.
- Interaction (MP x genotype): NOT FORMALLY TESTED - only pairwise * (vs vehicle) and # (vs WT) contrasts. Claimed genotype-dependent effects (steatosis, PPARg/C/EBPa opposite directions; serum lipids changed only in WT; larger lipolysis-protein decreases in WT) rest on separate within-genotype tests. Many other MP effects reported as same-direction "regardless of the mouse type". Authors compare WT findings to HFD + MP literature and attribute Lep KO differences to "the diversity of mechanisms that induce obesity" (Discussion) - interpretive, not tested.

## Author-acknowledged limitations (quotes + location)
- "it did not measure the distributions of the administered MPs within exact locations such as the hepatocytes, Kupffer cells, and sinusoids of liver tissue" (Discussion para 2).
- "the lack of clear analyses of MPs' kinetics, including their absorption, distribution, excretion in WT and Lep KO mice, should be considered as a drawback of our study" (Discussion para 2).
- "the study did not analyze the mechanism of action of MPs on inflammation, glucotoxicity, and glucagon regulation" (Conclusions).
- "it does not provide any results for a direct comparison between the diet-induced obesity model and the gene-deficient-induced obesity model" (Conclusions).
- Leptin: "additional studies are needed to prove the molecular mechanism" (Discussion).

## Methodological points to verify independently
- Sex of the experimental mice is never stated; were sexes balanced across the four groups?
- Effective n: legends say "five to six mice per group" though allocation was n = 6; Table 1 means/SDs appear consistent with fewer animals (reader arithmetic) - check how many mice contributed to each serum value and why any were dropped.
- MP x genotype: no two-way ANOVA or interaction term is described; are the Lep KO decreases in PPARg/C-EBPa themselves significant, and do WT and Lep KO responses differ significantly from each other?
- Technical duplicates ("analyzed twice"): averaged per mouse before testing, or treated as replicates?
- Dosing wording "once daily (0.5 mL/day) three times a week" is internally ambiguous; the dose rationale quotes ug/L while dosing is 100 ug/mL; no mg/kg body weight given.
- Text/number consistency: Results give LDL "+11.9%" while Table 1 shows 4 -> 8.5 mg/dL; Discussion mentions "45 days"/"90 days" though the design is 9 weeks, and quotes 0.1 um for a reference the Introduction cites as 1 um.
- Metabolomics: Abstract says the seven metabolites changed "in the serum" but Results/Methods describe liver; Methods 4.12 mixes HPLC assays with Chenomx NMR analysis without describing NMR acquisition. Which platform produced Figs 13-14, what n, and was any multiple-testing correction applied?
- Particle: fluorophore and surface chemistry not described; was the Fig. 17 characterisation performed on this batch; endotoxin not reported; were autofluorescence controls used for detecting particles in liver sections?
- Histology: was NAFLD scoring blinded, and can the inflammation and ballooning sub-scores be seen separately?
- Housing: cage allocation, litter structure and whether WT and Lep KO were co-housed are not reported; randomisation not reported.
- Time of day of necropsy and fasting state are not reported, which matters for hepatic Glu, glycogenolysis transcripts and leptin.
- RT-qPCR reference gene is not named in the main text (check Supplementary Table S1).
- Interpretation to check: increased insulin receptor, p-IRS-1, p-PI3K and p-Akt are read by the authors as increased insulin resistance; no glucose/insulin tolerance test or HOMA-IR was performed.

## Relevance to the team
A 2 x 2 (MP x genotype) PS-MP study in which obesity is genetic (Lep KO) rather than diet-induced, with no transcriptome: hepatic steatosis and PPARg/C/EBPa moved in OPPOSITE directions in WT versus Lep KO, and serum lipid increases occurred only in WT, yet nothing was tested as a formal interaction - all claims rest on within-genotype pairwise tests. It is a cautionary comparator for interpreting MP effects that depend on metabolic background, and it reports several NULLs (body weight, liver weight, adipose weight, food/water intake).
