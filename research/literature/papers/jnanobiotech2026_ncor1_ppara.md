## Biological question
Does oral exposure to polystyrene nanoplastics (PSNPs) exacerbate high-fat-diet-induced hepatic steatosis in mice, and is the mechanism impaired autophagic degradation of the corepressor NCoR1 leading to suppression of PPARalpha-driven fatty acid beta-oxidation? Stated hypothesis (Introduction): "we hypothesized that PSNPs exacerbate lipid accumulation in HFD-fed mice."

## Experimental design
- In vivo main study: 2x2 factorial, four groups, n = 10 per group, 90 days. Quote (Methods, Animals and experimental design): "Mice were randomly allocated into four experimental groups (n = 10 per group): ND: normal diet + daily oral gavage of equal volumes of ultrapure water (ddH2O); PSNPs: normal diet + 150 mg/kg.bw PSNPs; HFD: high-fat diet ... + ddH2O; HFD+PSNPs: high-fat diet + 150 mg/kg.bw PSNPs."
- Randomisation: stated ("randomly allocated"); also "two mice per group were randomly selected" for TEM and "Six randomly selected liver specimens per group" for lipid assays.
- Blinding: NOT stated anywhere. NAS scoring is described (steatosis 0-3, lobular inflammation 0-3, ballooning 0-2) but no blinding of the scorer is reported.
- Separate biodistribution experiment: single gavage of 150 mg/kg.bw red-fluorescent PSNPs, whole-body imaging at 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24 h, organs harvested at 0 and 24 h. Group sizes for this arm are NOT reported.
- In vitro: human L-02 hepatocytes, OA pre-load then PSNPs; plus a rapamycin co-treatment arm. Design is a lipid-loaded cell model, not a 2x2 factorial.
- Note an asymmetry: the omics are NOT run on all four groups. Transcriptomics and metabolomics compare ONLY HFD+PSNP vs HFD.

## Species/strain/sex/age; tissue(s)
Male C57BL/6J mice, 5-6 weeks old at the start (Vital River Laboratories, Beijing). Male only; the paper does not flag sex as a limitation. Housing: "12 h light/dark cycle, 23 +/- 1 C, 60-70% relative humidity" with ad libitum water. Number of mice per cage: not reported.
Tissues: liver (histology, Oil Red O, TEM, lipid biochemistry, ALT/AST measured IN LIVER HOMOGENATE not serum, RNA-seq, metabolomics, western blot, IHC, IF, immunoprecipitation); serum (inflammatory factors, Figure S1, not read); heart, lung, liver, spleen, kidney, intestine for the biodistribution arm only.
Cells: human L-02 "normal liver" line (Nanjing Keygen). No authentication or mycoplasma statement. Note L-02 is widely reported to be HeLa-contaminated in cell-line registries - worth verifying.

## Biological replicates per group (per assay)
- Body weight, food intake, fasting glucose, liver weight: n = 10 (Fig. 2 legend "n = 10"; Fig. 3a "n = 10").
- Histology (H&E, NAS), Oil Red O quantification, hepatic ALT/AST, hepatic TG/TC/FFA/FC, RT-qPCR, most western blots: n = 6 (Fig. 3 legend "(n = 6)"; Methods: "cryosections ... from six animals per group"; "Six liver samples per group were homogenized").
- RNA-seq: n = 4. "hepatic transcriptomic profiling was conducted on four liver specimens" (Methods, Transcriptomic analysis); Fig. 4a legend "(n = 4)". Only two groups sequenced (HFD and HFD+PSNP).
- Metabolomics: n = 6 per group, HFD and HFD+PSNP only (Methods: "(n = 6 per group)"; Fig. 4d "(n = 6)").
- TEM: "two mice per group were randomly selected" (Methods) - n = 2, descriptive only.
- IHC for NCoR1, LC3B IF quantification, NCoR1 nuclear IF, NCoR1 phospho-IP: n = 3 (Fig. 5e "n = 3"; Fig. 6b "n = 3"; Fig. 7a "n = 3"; Fig. 7c "n = 3").
- qPCR technical replicates: "Reactions were performed in triplicate for each biological sample (n = 3 technical replicates)" - note this is technical, not biological, replication.

## Polymer; particle size; supplier; surface/fluorescent label; characterisation; endotoxin
- Polymer: polystyrene nanoplastics (PSNPs). Two formulations: "red-fluorescent particles (excitation/emission: 620/680 nm) for in vivo distribution studies, and pristine particles for toxicity evaluation" (Methods). Importantly the 90-day toxicity study used PRISTINE (unlabelled) particles - the label was confined to the biodistribution arm, which avoids the label-confound that affects many MNP studies.
- Nominal size: 50 nm. Supplier: "PSNPs (50 nm) were obtained from DK Nanotechnology Co. (Beijing, China) as aqueous suspensions with an initial concentration of 50 mg/mL in sterile deionized water."
- Measured primary size (TEM, 500 particles, ImageJ): "an average diameter of 49.24 +/- 5.31 nm" (Results; Fig. 1b).
- Hydrodynamic size / zeta / PDI (Table 1, read): in H2O at 0 h, 53.35 +/- 0.99 nm, -30.83 +/- 4.06 mV, PDI 0.05 +/- 0.02; at 24 h, 53.14 +/- 0.32 nm, -25.47 +/- 1.12 mV, PDI 0.04 +/- 0.01. In DMEM at 0 h, 52.68 +/- 0.35 nm, -30.70 +/- 1.61 mV, PDI 0.06 +/- 0.01; at 24 h, 58.26 +/- 0.12 nm, -22.70 +/- 2.14 mV, PDI 0.14 +/- 0.00. So mild agglomeration and loss of surface charge over 24 h in serum-free DMEM, stable in water. The authors describe this as "favorable colloidal stability".
- Dispersion handling: "all nanoparticle suspensions were ultrasonicated for 30 min at 160 W and 20 kHz".
- ENDOTOXIN: not reported (searched Methods and whole text). Residual surfactant, monomer or additive leachate content: not reported. No unlabelled-vs-labelled biological comparison was needed since the toxicity study used pristine particles, but no comparison of this supplier's particles against a reference material is given either.

## Dose; route; vehicle; frequency; duration
- Dose: 150 mg/kg body weight per day. Route: oral gavage. Vehicle: ultrapure water (ddH2O); control groups gavaged with equal volumes of ddH2O (so gavage stress is matched across groups - a strength).
- Frequency/duration: "mice were orally administered PSNPs suspended in ddH2O daily for 90 consecutive days", after a 7-day acclimatisation.
- Dose justification (quote, Methods): "The murine dose was determined based on the upper range of estimated human microplastic ingestion (0.1-5 g/week/individual) ... a standardized interspecies dose conversion factor of 12.3 ... The resulting dose of 150 mg/kg.bw (for a 20 g mouse) corresponds to a human equivalent dose of approximately 5.0 g/week for a 60 kg adult". Note the Conclusion calls this "environmentally relevant concentrations" while the Methods describe it as the UPPER range of estimated human intake - these are not the same claim.
- Biodistribution arm: single gavage, 150 mg/kg.bw, red-fluorescent particles.
- In vitro: L-02 cells pre-loaded with 100 uM oleic acid for 24 h, then 25 ug/mL PSNPs for 24 h. Rapamycin arm: 100 nM rapamycin + 25 ug/mL PSNPs, 24 h.

## Diet / metabolic-stress model
- HFD: "high-fat diet (D12492, Research Diets Inc., USA; 459 kcal/100 g, 60% fat, 20% protein, 20% carbohydrate)" - the same 60% kcal lard diet as the companion PS-NP paper. Obesogenic steatosis model.
- Control: "normal diet" (ND). Brand, composition and % kcal fat NOT reported, and no statement that it is a purified low-fat diet matched to D12492. Point to verify.
- The model deliberately stops short of steatohepatitis: "The NAFLD Activity Score (NAS) indicated that none of the experimental groups met the diagnostic criteria for non-alcoholic steatohepatitis (NASH), and no inflammation was observed among groups" (Results). So this is a pure steatosis ("first hit") model, explicitly framed as such in the Discussion.
- In vitro lipotoxic stress: 100 uM oleic acid, 24 h (mono-unsaturated FA only, no palmitate).

Sequencing DEPTH is NOT reported (no read counts, no alignment rates, no library QC metrics anywhere) - a notable omission compared with the Adv Sci paper. Only HFD vs HFD+PSNP, n = 4.
- Untargeted metabolomics: UHPLC-QTOF-MS/MS plus GC-MS, MS-DIAL processing, annotation against HMDB, LipidMaps v2.3, METLIN, LuMet-Animal 3.0, LuMet-GC 5.0. n = 6, HFD vs HFD+PSNP only.
- RT-qPCR (liver), Gapdh reference, 2^-ddCt.
- Western blot (liver and L-02): ACSL3, CPT1A, ACADVL, HMGCS1, NCoR1, p62, LC3B, mTOR, p-mTOR, S6, p-S6; GAPDH loading control. Antibody list in Supplementary Table S1 (not read).
- Immunoprecipitation: NCoR1 pulled down, probed with a pan phospho-(Ser/Thr) antibody (no phospho-NCoR1-specific antibody available).
- Immunohistochemistry (NCoR1), immunofluorescence (LC3B in tissue; NCoR1/LC3B/LAMP1 colocalisation and BODIPY lipid droplets in L-02), confocal Nikon Ti2.
- Histology: H&E with NAS, Oil Red O, whole-slide scanning; TEM of liver ultrastructure.
- Biochemistry: hepatic TG, TC, FFA, FC; hepatic ALT and AST (tissue homogenate, normalised to protein - NOT serum transaminases); fasting blood glucose; serum inflammatory factors and fibrosis staining (Figure S1, not read).
- No lipidomics as such, no proteomics, no scRNA-seq, no spatial, no circadian/time-series sampling of any kind.

## Statistics & computational methods
- DE (RNA-seq): "Differential expression analysis was carried out with the DESeq2 package, applying statistical thresholds of adjusted p-value < 0.05 and |log2 Fold change| > 1". Pipeline: fastp (v1.0) -> HISAT2 -> FPKM normalisation and HTSeq-count. Result: "236 differentially expressed genes (DEGs), including 95 upregulated and 141 downregulated genes in the HFD+PSNP group compared to the HFD group" (Results).
- Metabolomics DEMs: "adjusted p-value < 0.05, variable importance in projection (VIP) > 1, and |log2 Fold change| > 1"; "102 differentially expressed metabolites (DEMs, 51 upregulated and 51 downregulated)".
- Pathway: KEGG enrichment of DEGs (Fig. 4b). The enrichment TOOL and the multiple-testing correction for enrichment are NOT reported (only "color indicates the p-value" in the legend). Reactome/GSEA not used.
- Phenotype statistics: "Two-way analysis of variance (ANOVA) was applied as appropriate. Differences were considered statistically significant at p < 0.05", GraphPad Prism 9. Figure legends consistently say "two-way ANOVA with a Tukey multiple comparison test".
- INTERACTION TESTING: the model is two-way ANOVA (diet x PSNPs), so an interaction term exists, but NO interaction F or p value is reported anywhere. All reported significance is PAIRWISE, and the legends define the comparisons explicitly: "'#' indicates statistically significant difference versus the ND control group ... '*' indicates a statistically significant difference versus the HFD control group". So the "synergy" claim rests on the HFD+PSNP vs HFD pairwise contrast, not on a tested interaction.
- The OMICS cannot test an interaction at all: only HFD+PSNP vs HFD was sequenced/profiled. The ND and ND+PSNP arms have no transcriptome or metabolome, so no diet x particle model is possible with these data.
- SD/SEM inconsistency: Methods state "Data are presented as mean +/- standard error of the mean (SEM)", but every figure legend states "mean +/- SD". One of the two is wrong; worth verifying before quoting any error bar.

## Main findings with effect directions and statistical evidence

1. Particles reach the liver. Whole-body fluorescence after a single gavage of 150 mg/kg.bw red-labelled PSNPs changed over 24 h and "PSNPs were clearly distributed in the liver" at 24 h (Fig. 1c,d). VALIDATED (in vivo optical imaging + ex vivo organ imaging), but qualitative - no quantification of hepatic particle burden, no comparison across groups, and the imaging used labelled particles whereas the 90-day study used pristine ones.
2. TEM showed PSNPs "localized in intracellular vacuoles" in hepatocytes after 90 days (Fig. 2h). VALIDATED (TEM) but n = 2 mice per group, descriptive only.
3. Food intake unaffected by PSNPs (Fig. 2b). Body weight: HFD+PSNP showed "transient body weight increases during the first six weeks relative to HFD-only controls", but "final body weights did not differ significantly" (Fig. 2c-e). VALIDATED (n = 10).
4. Fasting blood glucose: "HFD+PSNP co-exposure significantly elevated fasting blood glucose after 90 days compared to ND controls" (Fig. 2g). VALIDATED - but note the quoted comparison is vs ND, not vs HFD, so this is largely a diet effect.
5. NO liver injury and NO inflammation. Liver weight and liver/body ratio unchanged by PSNPs; hepatic ALT and AST "showed no significant differences among groups" (Fig. 3a,b); NAS did not reach NASH criteria and "no inflammation was observed among groups"; "neither serum inflammatory factors nor tissue fibrosis staining showed significant alterations (Figure S1)". VALIDATED (histology, enzymology; Figure S1 supplementary, not read). This is a clean negative for inflammation and fibrosis and the authors treat it as the point, not a failure.
6. Steatosis IS worsened by PSNPs on top of HFD: H&E showed "pronounced hepatocyte lipid accumulation in HFD+PSNP mice compared to HFD-only controls", and Oil Red O showed "larger and more numerous lipid droplets compared with HFD alone" (Fig. 3c,e,f). VALIDATED (histology + Oil Red O quantification, n = 6).
7. Hepatic lipids: "triglyceride (TG) levels were highest in the HFD+PSNP group, whereas total cholesterol (TC) and free fatty acid (FFA) levels remained unchanged"; free cholesterol (FC) was selectively increased in HFD-fed mice vs ND (Fig. 3g). VALIDATED (enzymatic assays, n = 6). So: TG UP, TC unchanged, FFA unchanged, FC up.
8. PSNPs alone (ND + PSNPs) produced NO hepatic phenotype. Stated in the Discussion: "this is the first study reporting that orally administered PSNPs exacerbate HFD-induced hepatic accumulation without phenotypically affecting ND-fed mice." This is a diet-dependent effect - important for the team.
9. Transcriptome: 236 DEGs (95 up, 141 down) in HFD+PSNP vs HFD (Figure S2a,b - supplementary, not read; counts quoted from Results text). OMICS-ONLY. Note the modest size of this DEG set and that it is a single pairwise contrast with n = 4.
10. KEGG enrichment: "significant suppression of the PPAR signaling pathway, which governs fatty acid beta-oxidation, along with associated steroid biosynthesis and terpenoid backbone biosynthesis pathways" (Fig. 4b). OMICS-ONLY/COMPUTATIONAL. Direction = SUPPRESSED, including the cholesterol/mevalonate arms.
11. Specific PPARalpha-pathway genes DOWN: "Acsl3, Hmgcs1, Fabp4, Fabp5, and Cyp4a31 - were markedly downregulated" (Fig. 4c). OMICS-ONLY at this stage. Note Cyp4a31 is a canonical PPARalpha target; Fabp4/Fabp5 are lipid-binding/transport genes; Hmgcs1 is the CYTOSOLIC HMG-CoA synthase (mevalonate/cholesterol pathway), not the ketogenic mitochondrial Hmgcs2 - see points to verify.
12. Metabolomics: 102 DEMs; accumulation of long-chain fatty acids ("tuberculostearic acid, sciadonic acid, and 13-methyltetradec-9-enoic acid") "along with elevated levels of fatty acylcarnitines, including 3-lcosa-8,11,14-trienoylcarnitine" (Fig. 4e). OMICS-ONLY. Authors read accumulated LCFAs + acylcarnitines as "impaired mitochondrial beta-oxidation" - a reasonable but indirect inference; no flux measurement was done.
13. PPARalpha target genes and proteins DOWN, validated: "mRNA levels of Acsl3, Cpt1a, Acox1, and Hmgcs1, as well as protein levels of ACSL3, CPT1A, and HMGCS1, were significantly decreased in the HFD+PSNP group compared to HFD controls" (Fig. 5b,c). VALIDATED (RT-qPCR n = 6 + western blot n = 6). Acadvl was assayed too (Fig. 5b,c list it) but the text does not state it changed - treat Acadvl/ACADVL as not clearly altered.
14. KEY DIRECTION FOR THE TEAM: PPARalpha signalling is SUPPRESSED, and FAO targets Cpt1a, Acox1, Acsl3 and Cyp4a31 go DOWN. The Discussion is explicit about this being the opposite of some other reports: "Given the conserved nature of PPARalpha signaling and the observed suppression (rather than activation) of this pathway, our results identify a novel mechanism". Note also that PPARalpha itself (Ppara transcript/PPARalpha protein) is NOT directly measured - the claim rests on target genes plus corepressor behaviour.
15. Lipogenesis and uptake NOT changed: "RT-qPCR analysis of genes involved in FFA synthesis, TG synthesis, and FFA uptake revealed no significant alterations, suggesting that PSNPs selectively impair fatty acid oxidation rather than lipid synthesis or uptake pathways (Figure S4)". VALIDATED by qPCR but the panel is supplementary and was not read; gene identities unverified. Note tension with finding 11 (Fabp4/Fabp5, uptake/transport genes, were among the downregulated DEGs).
16. NCoR1 protein UP: "a marked increase in NCoR1 protein expression in HFD+PSNP-treated mice compared to HFD controls" (western blot, Fig. 5d, n = 6), with IHC showing "increased NCoR1 accumulation in hepatocytes" (Fig. 5e, n = 3). VALIDATED (western blot + IHC).
17. Autophagy suppressed post-translationally: LC3B immunofluorescence reduced in HFD+PSNP liver (Fig. 6a,b, n = 3); "While the Lc3b mRNA levels remained unchanged, LC3B protein levels were substantially decreased" (Fig. 6c,d). VALIDATED (IF + qPCR + western blot). p62 was blotted (Fig. 6d) but its direction is not stated in the text - a gap, since p62 direction is what distinguishes blocked flux from reduced initiation.
18. NCoR1 is an autophagy-lysosome substrate: "immunofluorescence co-staining demonstrated colocalization of NCoR1 with both autophagosomes and lysosomes" in L-02 cells (Fig. 6e). VALIDATED by colocalisation only - this is correlative imaging, not a degradation assay (no cycloheximide chase, no bafilomycin/chloroquine flux comparison, no Atg7 knockdown in this paper).
19. NCoR1 phosphorylation UP: NCoR1 immunoprecipitated then probed with a pan phospho-(Ser/Thr) antibody showed "significantly increased phosphorylation of NCoR1 in HFD+PSNP liver samples" (Fig. 7a, n = 3). VALIDATED by IP-western, but with an acknowledged workaround: "due to the unavailability of specific phospho-NCoR1 antibodies". No phosphosite was mapped.
20. mTOR/S6K2 activation: p-mTOR increased; S6K2 activity was inferred from p-S6 because "In the absence of S6K2-specific phospho-antibodies, increased S6 phosphorylation was used as a surrogate marker for S6K2 activity" (Fig. 7b). VALIDATED for mTOR and S6, INFERRED for S6K2 specifically. p-S6 does not distinguish S6K1 from S6K2, so the "S6K2" in the title of the mechanism is not directly demonstrated here.
21. NCoR1 nuclear translocation increased (immunostaining, Fig. 7c, n = 3). VALIDATED by imaging; no subcellular fractionation western blot is shown in the main figures for the in vivo samples (the Discussion mentions a "Nuclear-cytoplasmic Western blot" but attributes it to Figure S5, the cell experiment).
22. RAPAMYCIN RESCUE (the only causal manipulation in the paper, and it is in vitro): "rapamycin treatment abolished PSNP-induced nuclear accumulation of NCoR1 and alleviated intracellular lipid deposition ... rapamycin restored the expression of key fatty acid oxidation proteins downregulated by PSNPs (Figure S5a-c)". VALIDATED by PHARMACOLOGICAL RESCUE, IN VITRO ONLY, in L-02 cells, and the data sit entirely in a supplementary figure that was not read. The authors call this "direct pharmacological evidence".
23. NOT DONE anywhere in this paper: no genetic manipulation of any kind - no Ncor1 knockdown/knockout, no liver-specific Ncor1 deletion, no Ppara knockout, no Atg7 or autophagy-gene manipulation, no rescue by NCoR1 silencing, no PPARalpha agonist (e.g. fenofibrate) challenge. No ChIP or co-IP of NCoR1 with PPARalpha (the NCoR1-PPARalpha interaction central to the model is asserted from literature, not measured here). No in vivo rescue of any kind. No FAO flux or isotope tracing. The causal chain PSNP -> autophagy block -> NCoR1 stabilisation -> PPARalpha repression -> steatosis is therefore supported by correlation at every in vivo step plus one in vitro mTOR-inhibitor experiment.

## Pathway-theme directions
- Lipid metabolism / lipogenesis: hepatic TG UP, lipid droplets larger and more numerous (VALIDATED). But de novo lipogenesis and uptake genes were explicitly UNCHANGED (Figure S4, not read) - the authors stress that steatosis here is a catabolic defect, not a synthetic one. Contrast with the Adv Sci PS-NP paper, where Fasn/Srebp1c/Lxra went UP.
- PPARalpha / FAO: SUPPRESSED. This is the paper's central claim and the direction the team needs. Down at mRNA and protein: Acsl3/ACSL3, Cpt1a/CPT1A, Acox1, Hmgcs1/HMGCS1 (VALIDATED by qPCR + western blot, n = 6); Cyp4a31, Fabp4, Fabp5 down in the DEG set (OMICS-ONLY). KEGG "PPAR signaling pathway" enriched as suppressed. Metabolomics shows LCFA and acylcarnitine ACCUMULATION, consistent with a beta-oxidation block (OMICS-ONLY). Ppara itself was not measured.
- Cholesterol synthesis: DOWN / suppressed. "steroid biosynthesis and terpenoid backbone biosynthesis pathways" were among the suppressed enrichments (OMICS-ONLY), and HMGCS1 protein was significantly decreased (VALIDATED). Meanwhile hepatic total cholesterol was UNCHANGED and free cholesterol went UP in HFD groups vs ND. Note the paper counts HMGCS1 as a PPARalpha/FAO readout, which is arguable.
- Inflammation: NO CHANGE, and this is a positive design feature here. NAS inflammation component zero across groups, serum inflammatory factors unchanged (Figure S1, not read), ALT/AST unchanged. No Tnf/Il1b/Il6/NLRP3/macrophage marker data. The authors frame it with the multiple-hit model: PSNP+HFD is "a potent 'first hit'" producing steatosis without the second (inflammatory) hit.
- Oxidative stress: NOT STUDIED AT ALL. No ROS, MDA, SOD, GSH, Nrf2 or Hmox1 measurement in vivo or in vitro. Do not cite this paper for MNP oxidative stress.
- Mitochondrial function: inferred only. No membrane potential, no ATP, no respirometry, no mitochondrial morphology quantification (TEM was used to find particles, not to score mitochondria). "Impaired mitochondrial beta-oxidation" is an inference from CPT1A/ACOX1 protein loss plus acylcarnitine accumulation - OMICS + protein, no functional assay.
- Fibrosis: NO CHANGE. "tissue fibrosis staining showed no significant alterations (Figure S1)" (supplementary, not read).
- Circadian: NOT STUDIED. No clock genes, no time-of-sampling information at all. The Methods state only that mice were "fasted overnight and anesthetized" before collection - no Zeitgeber time, no light-cycle position for tissue harvest is reported anywhere. See points to verify.
- Autophagy: SUPPRESSED (LC3B protein and LC3B-positive area down, Lc3b mRNA unchanged). p62 direction not reported in text.
- mTOR: ACTIVATED (p-mTOR up, p-S6 up).
- MNP x diet interaction: claimed as "synergistic" in the Discussion ("a synergistic amplification of hepatic lipid accumulation when HFD was combined with oral PSNP exposure, exceeding the effects of either factor alone") but NOT formally tested as an interaction. The design is a proper 2x2 with two-way ANOVA, yet only pairwise Tukey comparisons vs ND and vs HFD are reported; no interaction F/p appears anywhere. The strongest honest statement the data support is: PSNPs changed the liver phenotype only on the HFD background and not on ND - which is consistent with, but not proof of, a statistical interaction.

## Author-acknowledged limitations (quotes + location)
The paper has no limitations section; two limitations appear in the last paragraph of the Discussion:
1. "First, while we identify mTOR as a key regulator, the contributions of parallel signaling pathways and the precise phosphorylation sites on NCoR1 require further mapping."
2. "Second, the specific autophagic receptor responsible for NCoR1 recognition remains unidentified."
Methodological constraints conceded in passing in the Results, which function as limitations:
3. "due to the unavailability of specific phospho-NCoR1 antibodies, immunoprecipitation of NCoR1 followed by probing with a pan-phosphorylation antibody was conducted" (Results, Fig. 7a).
4. "In the absence of S6K2-specific phospho-antibodies, increased S6 phosphorylation was used as a surrogate marker for S6K2 activity" (Results, Fig. 7b).
5. Species caveat for the pathway: "Although species differences in PPARalpha biology exist, its fundamental role in fatty acid oxidation is evolutionarily conserved in humans" (Discussion).
NOT acknowledged anywhere: male-only design, absence of genetic validation, lack of RNA-seq QC/depth reporting, omics restricted to two of four groups, no blinding, no circadian/sampling-time control, SEM-vs-SD inconsistency.

## Methodological points to verify independently
- Omics asymmetry: transcriptomics and metabolomics cover only HFD and HFD+PSNP. Can any statement about PSNP effects "on the normal diet background" be made from omics? No. Verify whether ND and ND+PSNP libraries exist anywhere (they are not mentioned).
- RNA-seq n = 4 vs phenotype n = 10 / assay n = 6: which 4 animals per group, and chosen how? Not stated.
- RNA-seq QC entirely missing: no read depth, no mapping rate, no library batch statement, no mention of whether all samples were prepared/sequenced together. For a team whose own PE effects were depth- and quality-collinear, this is the key unverifiable item in this paper.
- Interaction: the model is two-way ANOVA; are interaction F/p values available in source data? All printed significance is pairwise vs ND or vs HFD.
- SEM vs SD: Methods say SEM, every legend says SD. Which is it? This changes how large the reported effects look.
- Sampling time / circadian: no ZT or clock-time of sacrifice is reported, and mice were "fasted overnight" before collection. Overnight fasting in mice removes the nocturnal feeding bout and by itself strongly induces hepatic PPARalpha and FAO genes - so all groups sit in a fasted, PPARalpha-primed state. Verify whether fasting duration was identical across groups and whether the fasting choice could interact with the particle effect.
- ALT/AST were measured in LIVER HOMOGENATE normalised to protein, not in serum (Methods). That is an unusual readout for hepatocellular injury; the "no liver injury" conclusion should be checked against this.
- Blinding: not reported for NAS scoring, Oil Red O quantification, IHC/IF quantification or any image analysis. NAS in particular is subjective.
- n = 3 for the mechanistic imaging and the phospho-IP (Figs 5e, 6b, 7a, 7c), and n = 2 for TEM. The mechanistic core of the paper runs on the smallest group sizes in the study.
- Cage/housing: mice per cage not reported. Gavage dosing is individual, which is a real advantage over drinking-water exposure for dose control, but diet is still shared at cage level.
- Control diet composition not reported ("normal diet"); not stated to be a purified low-fat match to D12492.
- L-02 cell identity: widely flagged as HeLa-contaminated in cell-line databases; no STR authentication or mycoplasma statement in Methods. The in vitro mechanism (including the rapamycin rescue) rests on this line.
- The rapamycin rescue is the only causal experiment and exists only in Figure S5, which was not read. Verify its controls: was rapamycin alone (without PSNPs) included? Rapamycin both inhibits mTOR and induces autophagy, so it could rescue via the autophagy arm rather than the NCoR1-phosphorylation arm - the two mechanisms the paper proposes are not separated by this experiment.
- NCoR1-PPARalpha interaction is never measured (no co-IP, no ChIP on FAO gene promoters). Verify whether any direct interaction or promoter-occupancy data exist.
- p62 direction is not stated although p62 was blotted (Fig. 6d) - needed to tell reduced autophagosome formation from blocked flux. No flux assay (bafilomycin/chloroquine, tandem mCherry-GFP-LC3) was performed.
- Hmgcs1 vs Hmgcs2: the paper treats HMGCS1 (cytosolic, mevalonate/cholesterol) as a PPARalpha fatty-acid-oxidation target. The ketogenic PPARalpha target is HMGCS2. Verify which gene/protein was actually measured and whether the antibody and primers are isoform-specific (Supplementary Tables S1, S2, not read).
- "Environmentally relevant" vs human-equivalent upper-range dose: 150 mg/kg/day is derived from the UPPER end of estimated human intake with a 12.3x allometric factor. The Conclusion's phrase "environmentally relevant concentrations" overstates this; verify before reusing the dose framing.
- Endotoxin not reported for a 90-day oral exposure at 150 mg/kg/day - a large cumulative particle mass.

## Relevance to the team
Directly relevant to the PPARalpha direction question: this paper reports PPARalpha signalling SUPPRESSED by nanoplastic on an HFD background, with Cpt1a, Acox1, Acsl3 and Cyp4a31 DOWN at mRNA and ACSL3/CPT1A/HMGCS1 down at protein - the opposite sign to the Ppara induction Jung et al. 2026 report for PE. The differences that could explain the discrepancy are all documented here and should be carried into any comparison: polymer (PS vs PE), size (50 nm vs micro), route (daily gavage of a defined 150 mg/kg dose vs dietary admixture), duration (90 days), and an overnight fast before sacrifice that pre-activates PPARalpha in every group. It also offers a methodological contrast for the team's interaction question: a 2x2 design whose omics arm covers only two of the four cells, so its "synergy" claim is a pairwise HFD+PSNP vs HFD result with no interaction statistic, and it reports no RNA-seq depth or batch information at all. Finally, it is a clean steatosis-without-inflammation-or-fibrosis model, which bounds what it can say about MASH progression.
