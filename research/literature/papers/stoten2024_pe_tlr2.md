## Main findings with effect directions and statistical evidence

- "MPs significantly reduced the liver organ coefficient, leading to liver cell injury and impaired function." Direction: liver/body weight ratio DOWN. Label: stated as experimental result; assay NOT VERIFIED.

## Relevance to the team
One of only two **polyethylene** oral-exposure mouse liver studies identified (with Djouina 2023). It reports PE-associated liver injury, oxidative stress, inflammation and fibrosis markers attributed to gut dysbiosis — consistent in direction with Djouina's "indirect, gut-mediated" interpretation. Because only the abstract could be read, it should be cited in the review as **background direction only**, never as a source of numbers, and it contributes no reanalysable data.
