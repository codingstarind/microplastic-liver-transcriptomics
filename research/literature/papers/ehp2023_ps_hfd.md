## Biological question
Whether oral PS-MP exposure has larger intestinal/metabolic effects under HFD-induced "leaky gut" than under a normal diet. Introduction: "we hypothesized that the toxicity of MPs might be more marked in LGS induced by a high-fat diet than under a normal diet." Focus: small intestine (jejunum) barrier, innate immune cells (ILC1/ILC3, M1/M2), microbiota, SCFA, and downstream glucose tolerance and liver (NAFLD).

## Experimental design (groups, controls, randomisation/blinding if stated)
- 2x2 factorial layout: ND, ND+MP, HFD, HFD+MP (Methods "Mice": "The mice were divided into four groups (n=10 per group) according to diet and whether or not they were given MPs.").
- Exposure 8 to 12 wk of age (4 wk); pair feeding: "equal amounts of feed and water were supplied for pair feeding"; intakes "normalized based on the lowest intake (group fed a normal diet with MPs added)".
- Housing: "One mouse was kept per cage" (Methods "Mice") - i.e. cage = animal, single-housed.
- Animals: "Littermate mice that were born in Shimizu Laboratory Supplies were used".
- Sacrifice after overnight fast at 12 wk; iPGTT 2 d and ITT 3 d before sacrifice, FITC-dextran 5 d before sacrifice (Methods).
- In vitro arm: MODE-K murine intestinal epithelial cells +/- MP (1,000 ug/L), palmitic acid (200 uM), IL-22 (50 ng/mL), 24 h.
- Randomisation: not reported (searched Methods for "random"). Blinding of histology/NAS scoring: not reported (searched "blind").

## Species/strain/sex/age; tissue(s)
- Mouse, C57BL/6J, male only: "We purchased 7-wk-old C57BL/6J (wild type) male mice from Shimizu Laboratory Supplies" (Methods, Mice). Diet/MP from 8 wk, euthanised at 12 wk.
- Tissues: jejunum/small intestine (histology, Muc2 IHC, lamina propria flow cytometry, qPCR), small-intestinal feces (palmitic acid, SCFA), "frozen appendicular fecal samples" for 16S, serum, liver (H&E, Oil Red O, NAS, palmitic acid, qPCR). No liver transcriptomics.
- In vitro: MODE-K murine intestinal epithelial cell line ("sex: female").

## Biological replicates per group
- Body weight, food/water intake: n=10 (Fig. 1B-D legend).
- iPGTT and ITT: n=5 each; Methods state "iPGTT and ITT were performed in different mice"; Fig. 1E-H legend "(n=5)".
- FITC-dextran permeability: n=10 (Methods, Fig. 1I).
- Serum biochemistry (ALT, TG, NEFA, LDL-C): n=10 (Methods, Blood Biochemistry).
- Jejunal histology / Muc2 / GFP area / flow cytometry: n=10 (Fig. 2 legend).
- Fecal palmitic acid and SCFAs: n=10 (Fig. 3 legend).
- Liver weight, NAS, Oil Red O, hepatic palmitic acid: n=10 (Fig. 4 legend).
- qPCR (jejunum and liver): AMBIGUOUS. Methods say "Expression levels from six mice from each group were determined, and RT-PCR was performed in triplicate for each sample (n=10)"; Table 1 note says "(n=10)". Whether n is 6 or 10 mice is unclear.
- 16S rRNA sequencing: n=6 (Fig. 5B-G legend).
- MODE-K experiments: Methods say "(n=6)"; Figure 6 legend says "(n=10)" - inconsistent.

## Polymer; particle size; supplier; label; characterisation; endotoxin
- Polymer/label/supplier (Methods, Mice): "carboxyl group-modified fluorescent PS particles (F-K1 050; 0.45-0.53 um polystyrene COOH; Green Fluorescent Protein (GFP) fluorescence; Merck)". So polystyrene, COOH surface-modified, green-fluorescent, nominal 0.45-0.53 um (sub-micron, i.e. nanoplastic/small MP range).
- Measured size distribution: not reported (no DLS/SEM/zeta potential in Methods).
- Characterisation performed: only dispersion stability in drinking water - water sonicated 20 kHz 15 min, jugs foil-wrapped, water changed every 3 d; 200 uL sampled on days 0,1,2 and particles counted by fluorescence microscopy plus fluorescence intensity, repeated 3x; "There were no statistically significant differences in the number and fluorescence intensity of MPs at the three time points" (Results, Verification of MP Uniformity; Figure S3).
- Endotoxin / LPS contamination of particles: not reported (searched full text for "endotoxin"; the only hit is background discussion of portal LPS influx in the Introduction).
- Leachables/additives, particle agglomeration in water, actual ingested particle number: not reported.

## Dose; route; vehicle; frequency; duration
- "MPs ... were dissolved in water at 1,000 ug/L, and water was provided ad libitum" (Methods, Mice). Route: oral, via drinking water. Vehicle: drinking water (sonicated). Frequency: continuous ad libitum, water replaced every 3 d.
- Duration: 4 wk (8 to 12 wk of age). Mice were pair-fed/pair-watered, so intake was equalised to the lowest-consuming group; absolute mg/kg/d dose is not reported.
- Single dose level only; authors note in the Discussion that "the evaluation of dose response and verification with other size and concentration combinations of microplastics has not been conducted".

## Diet / metabolic-stress model
- ND: "normal diet (ND; 345 kcal/100 g, fat kcal 4.6%; CLEA)".
- HFD: "high-fat diet [HFD; 459 kcal/100 g, 20% protein, 20% carbohydrate, and 60% fat (lard); D12492, Research Diets Inc.]" for 4 wk from 8 wk of age.
- Note: the ND is a chow (CLEA), NOT the matched low-fat purified control (e.g. D12450) for D12492, so diet differs in more than fat content. Model is HFD-induced obesity/NAFLD, 4 wk only; NAS was 0 in both ND groups.

## Data types
- No RNA-seq / microarray anywhere in this paper. Gene expression is TaqMan RT-qPCR only (jejunum: Tnfa, Il6, Il1b, Il22, Ffar2, Ffar3, Cd36, Sglt1/"Sglt2" in one sentence, Muc2; liver: Tnfa, Il6, Il1b, Scd1, Elovl6, Fasn), normalised to Gapdh, 2^-ddCt, ND set to 1.0.
- 16S rRNA amplicon sequencing (V3-V4, primers 341F/806R, Illumina MiSeq, Macrogen).
- GC-MS targeted metabolite measurement: palmitic acid in serum, feces, liver (Agilent 7890B/7000D); SCFAs (acetic, propanoic, butanoic) in feces by SPE-GC-MS.
- Histology: jejunum H&E (villus height/width, crypt depth), PAS (goblet cells), Muc2 immunofluorescence, GFP-positive area for MP deposition; liver H&E + Oil Red O + NAFLD activity score (NAS; steatosis 0-3, lobular inflammation 0-2, ballooning 0-2, fibrosis 0-4).
- Flow cytometry of small-intestinal lamina propria (ILC1, ILC3, T-bet+ ILC3, M1/M2 macrophages).
- Serum biochemistry: ALT, TG, NEFA, LDL-cholesterol.
- Functional tests: iPGTT (2 g/kg), ITT (0.5 U/kg in Methods, "0.75U/kg" in the Fig. 1 legend - INCONSISTENT), FITC-dextran permeability.
- In vitro: MODE-K Muc2 qPCR and MP accumulation (fluorescence).

## Statistics & computational methods
- "The data were analyzed using JMP software (version 13.0 ...). One-way ANOVA with Holm-Sidak's multiple comparisons test was used to compare the results of different groups. Statistical significance was set at p<0.05." (Methods, Statistical Analysis). Figures made in GraphPad Prism 9.
- CRITICAL FOR THE TEAM: the 2x2 design was analysed as a ONE-WAY ANOVA over four groups with pairwise multiple comparisons. There is NO two-way ANOVA, NO MP x diet interaction term, and no reported interaction p-value anywhere in the Methods, Results, Tables 1-2 or figure legends (searched for "interaction", "two-way"). All "MP effects only under HFD" statements are based on pairwise contrasts (Table 1 footnote: "*, p<0.05 vs. ND; +, p<0.05 vs. ND+MP; ++, p<0.05 vs. HFD group").
- No multiple-testing correction beyond the within-ANOVA Holm-Sidak step; no adjustment across the many separate outcome families (histology, qPCR panel, microbiome, metabolites).
- 16S: QIIME 1.9.1, quality filter dropping barcodes/primers scoring <75%, UCLUST 97% OTUs, BLAST/UNITE 2017 + Greengenes taxonomy; alpha diversity Chao1, Shannon, Gini-Simpson; phylum abundances by one-way ANOVA + Holm-Sidak; LEfSe (LDA) with Wilcoxon p<0.05 and LDA effect-size threshold 2 - LEfSe was run separately within each diet (ND vs ND+MP, and HFD vs HFD+MP), again not as an interaction.
- No power calculation, no exclusion criteria, no mixed model, no covariates reported.

## Main findings with effect directions and statistical evidence
All statistics below are PAIRWISE (one-way ANOVA + Holm-Sidak), never a formal interaction test.

NULL / no-difference results (as important as the positives):
- Body weight: no MP effect in either diet - "We observed no difference in the body weight of mice fed a ND and a ND with MPs added and of mice fed a HFD and a HFD with MPs added" (Results, Fig. 1B). VALIDATED (direct measurement).
- Food and water intake: "Intake of food and water was not different among the four groups" (Fig. 1C,D) - but note intakes were deliberately pair-fed/normalised, so this is partly by design.
- Relative liver weight (liver/body): "the ratio of liver weight to body weight did not differ significantly between ND- and HFD-fed mice, with or without exposure to MPs" (Fig. 4B). Absolute liver weight was LOWER in HFD+MP vs HFD (Fig. 4A) - direction opposite to the steatosis finding, unexplained by the authors.
- ITT: no difference ND vs ND+MP (Fig. 1G,H).
- Under ND, essentially the whole panel was null: serum ALT/TG/NEFA/LDL-C/palmitate (Fig. S4A-E), villus height/width, crypt depth, ILC1, T-bet+ ILC3, M1/M2, ILC3, fecal palmitate and SCFAs, liver NAS (0 in both ND groups), Oil Red O area, hepatic palmitate, and all 15 qPCR genes (Table 1: ND+MP values 0.86-1.22x ND, none starred).
- Microbiome diversity under ND: OTU number and Chao1/Shannon/Gini-Simpson "there was no difference between ND and mice fed the ND with MPs" (Fig. 5B-E).
- Phylum-level ANOVA p-values (Table 2) show NO significant ND vs ND+MP difference for any phylum (e.g. Bacteroidetes p=0.859, Proteobacteria p=0.995).
- In MODE-K cells, MP alone did not change Muc2: "The expression of Muc2 was not different between control group and MP alone group" (Fig. 6A) - the decrease needed palmitic acid.

Positive findings, HFD background (MP added on top of HFD):
- Glucose: iPGTT blood glucose/AUC higher in BOTH ND+MP and HFD+MP vs their diet controls (Fig. 1E,F) - this is the one readout where MP acted under ND too. ITT impaired only in HFD+MP vs HFD (Fig. 1G,H). VALIDATED (physiological test), n=5.
- Intestinal permeability (FITC-dextran): higher in HFD and HFD+MP; "plasma GFP expression was significantly higher in the mice fed an HFD that included MPs than in HFD-fed mice" (Fig. 1I, S3D-E). VALIDATED.
- Jejunal histology: villus height and width LOWER and crypt depth HIGHER in HFD+MP vs HFD; goblet cells (PAS+) and Muc2 mucus-layer thickness LOWER with MP in BOTH diets ("in mice fed the ND containing MPs and the those fed HFD containing MPs was significantly lower than in ND and HFD mice, respectively", Fig. 2E,F) - another readout where MP acted under ND. VALIDATED (histology/IHC).
- MP deposition in mucosa (GFP+ area): higher only in HFD+MP vs HFD; no ND vs ND+MP difference (Fig. 2G). VALIDATED (imaging).
- Innate immunity: ILC1/CD45+, T-bet+ ILC3/CD45+ and M1/M2 ratio HIGHER, ILC3/CD45+ LOWER in HFD+MP vs HFD; no differences ND vs ND+MP (Fig. 2H-K). VALIDATED (flow cytometry).
- Fecal metabolites: palmitic acid LOWER and acetic/propanoic/butanoic acid LOWER in HFD+MP vs HFD; no ND vs ND+MP change (Fig. 3A-D). VALIDATED (GC-MS).

LIVER findings (most relevant to the team):
- NAFLD activity score: "The NAS was zero in ND-fed mice, with and without exposure to MPs, whereas NAS in mice fed the HFD containing MPs was higher than that in mice fed the HFD without MPs" (Results; Fig. 4D). VALIDATED (histology). Note NAS includes a fibrosis sub-score but no separate fibrosis staining (no Sirius red/Masson) is reported.
- Oil Red O area: higher in HFD+MP vs HFD; no difference ND vs ND+MP (Fig. 4E). Authors add in the Discussion that "Red O staining area of the liver was higher in the group fed the ND with MPs than in the ND-only group, although not significantly" - i.e. a non-significant trend under ND.
- Hepatic palmitic acid: higher in HFD+MP vs HFD, unchanged ND vs ND+MP (Fig. 4F, GC-MS). VALIDATED.
- Hepatic qPCR (Table 1, mean (SD), ND=1):
  Tnfa 1 (0.21) / ND+MP 1.05 (0.28) / HFD 2.44 (0.83)* / HFD+MP 5.93 (1.18)*,+,++
  Il6 1 (0.19) / 1.01 (0.24) / 2.22 (0.36)* / 5.51 (0.50)*,+,++
  Il1b 1 (0.19) / 1.01 (0.24) / 1.66 (0.23)* / 6.17 (1.72)*,+,++
  Scd1 1 (0.12) / 1.16 (0.09) / 1.22 (0.20)* / 3.03 (0.27)*,+,++
  Elovl6 1 (0.12) / 1.16 (0.09) / 1.44 (0.24)* / 4.28 (0.95)*,+,++
  Fasn 1 (0.12) / 1.16 (0.09) / 2.05 (0.09)* / 9.82 (0.90)*,+,++
  (++ = p<0.05 vs HFD). So hepatic lipogenic (Scd1, Elovl6, Fasn) and inflammatory (Tnfa, Il6, Il1b) genes are UP with MP on HFD and UNCHANGED with MP on ND. VALIDATED by qPCR (but qPCR is the only molecular readout; no protein/western confirmation for liver genes).
  Oddity to flag: Scd1, Elovl6 and Fasn have identical ND and ND+MP means/SDs (1 (0.12) and 1.16 (0.09)) across three different genes - worth checking against Table S2.
- Jejunal qPCR (Table 1): Tnfa, Il6, Il1b, Ffar2, Ffar3, Cd36 (1.47 -> 7.63), Sglt1 (2.31 -> 6.11) UP and Il22 (0.47 -> 0.27), Muc2 (0.48 -> 0.18) DOWN in HFD+MP vs HFD; all ND vs ND+MP comparisons null.

Microbiome:
- HFD+MP vs HFD: Bacteroidetes DOWN (49.77% -> 29.12%, p=0.026), Proteobacteria UP (14.66% -> 44.98%, p=0.001), Deferribacteres UP (4.90% -> 8.65%, p=0.035) (Table 2); Firmicutes NS (p=0.220). Chao1/Shannon/Gini-Simpson LOWER in HFD+MP vs HFD. OMICS-ONLY (16S amplicon, no gnotobiotic/transfer validation in this paper).
- LEfSe: ND+MP enriched Proteobacteria, Parasutterella, Sutterellaceae, Betaproteobacteria and depleted Bacteroidales/Bacteroidia/Bacteroidetes/Tannerella (so MP did alter taxa under ND even though diversity and phenotype did not change); HFD+MP enriched Desulfovibrio and depleted Prevotellaceae, Bacteroidales, Bacteroidia, Bacteroidetes, Alloprevotella, Ruminococcaceae. OMICS-ONLY.

Mechanistic in vitro arm:
- Muc2 mRNA in MODE-K reduced by palmitic acid and by PA+MP (not by MP alone); IL-22 restored Muc2 in both PA and PA+MP groups (Fig. 6A,B). MP accumulation in cells higher with PA than MP alone, and reduced by IL-22 (Fig. 6C,D). VALIDATED in vitro (qPCR + fluorescence), but cell-line only, no in vivo IL-22 intervention, so the IL-22/SCFA mechanism in mice remains inferential.

## Pathway-theme directions
- Lipid metabolism / lipogenesis: hepatic Scd1, Elovl6, Fasn UP in HFD+MP vs HFD (3.0x, 4.3x, 9.8x of ND); unchanged by MP under ND. Intestinal Cd36 (long-chain FA transporter) UP 7.63x in HFD+MP. Hepatic and serum palmitic acid UP, fecal palmitate DOWN (interpreted as increased absorption). Srebf1, Acly, Pparg: not measured.
- PPARalpha / fatty-acid oxidation (Ppara, Cpt1a, Acox1, Cyp4a): NOT MEASURED in this paper. No FAO readout at all.
- Cholesterol synthesis: not measured in liver; serum LDL-cholesterol higher in HFD+MP vs HFD (Fig. S4D, value not read). Hmgcr etc. not measured.
- Inflammation: hepatic Tnfa, Il6, Il1b UP (2.4-6.2x) in HFD+MP; intestinal Tnfa/Il6/Il1b UP, Il22 DOWN; lamina propria ILC1 and M1/M2 UP, ILC3 DOWN. NF-kB, NLRP3, F4/80 or Cd68 liver macrophage markers: not measured in liver.
- Oxidative stress (ROS, MDA, SOD, GSH, Nrf2, Hmox1): NOT MEASURED anywhere (searched text).
- Mitochondrial function: NOT MEASURED.
- Fibrosis: only as the fibrosis sub-score inside NAS; no dedicated fibrosis stain or Col1a1/Acta2 expression. At 4 wk HFD, fibrosis is likely minimal.
- Circadian genes: NOT MEASURED; time of day of sacrifice not stated (only "after an overnight fast").
- MP x diet interaction: claimed verbally and repeatedly ("MP induced metabolic disturbances, such as diabetes and NAFLD, only in mice fed a high-fat diet", Abstract Discussion) but NEVER formally tested. No two-way ANOVA, no interaction term, no interaction p-value. The claim rests on "significant in HFD+MP vs HFD, not significant in ND+MP vs ND", which is the difference-of-significance fallacy. Two readouts (iPGTT glucose, goblet cells/Muc2 thickness) actually did show MP effects under ND, and the authors themselves describe non-significant ND trends for serum lipids and Oil Red O.

## Author-acknowledged limitations
- "There was a limitation of this study. In iPGTT, we did not measure the plasma insulin levels. If we had had the data, more accurate assessment of insulin resistance would have been possible." (Discussion, penultimate paragraph).
- "In addition, the evaluation of dose response and verification with other size and concentration combinations of microplastics has not been conducted, and this is a future research topic." (Discussion, same paragraph).
- Implicit acknowledgement of a power limitation: ND+MP serum lipids were higher "although the difference was not statistically significant in the present sample size" (Discussion).
- Measurement caveat acknowledged in Methods: "It was difficult to separate the FITC signal from FITC-dextran and GFP signal from GFP-MP using absorbance spectrometry" - the permeability index is a before/after subtraction, not a clean measure.

## Methodological points to verify independently
(Phrased as questions, not accusations.)
1. Interaction: the design is 2x2 but the analysis is one-way ANOVA over 4 groups. Does Table S2 (summary data) allow a reader to recompute a two-way ANOVA MP x diet interaction? Would the liver qPCR effects survive a formal interaction test?
2. n per assay: Methods say qPCR used "six mice from each group" while Table 1 says n=10; MODE-K is n=6 in Methods and n=10 in Fig. 6. Which is the biological n, and are the qPCR triplicates technical replicates (they should not inflate n)?
3. ITT dose: 0.5 U/kg (Methods) vs 0.75 U/kg (Fig. 1 legend). Which was used?
4. Diet control matching: ND is CLEA chow (4.6% kcal fat) vs HFD D12492 (60% kcal lard). Grain-based chow vs purified lard diet differ in fibre, sucrose, phytoestrogens and micronutrients - how much of the "HFD" effect, and of the MP x diet contrast, could be diet-matrix rather than fat?
5. Pair feeding to the lowest-intake group means all mice were mildly restricted relative to ad libitum; does this restriction interact with MP effects, and were MP-dosed mice drinking the same volume (hence the same particle dose)?
6. Particle dose: concentration is given (1,000 ug/L in water) but not particles/day or ug/kg body weight/day. Can a per-mouse dose be derived from the reported water intake (~6.4-7.4 g/d)?
7. Particle characterisation: no measured size distribution, zeta potential, agglomeration state in drinking water, or endotoxin assay. COOH-modified sub-micron PS beads are often supplied with surfactant/azide - was the suspension washed, and could vehicle components contribute?
8. Fluorescence: GFP-labelled beads were also used as the permeability tracer background and as the tissue "deposition" measure; dye leaching from beads is a known artefact. Was free dye excluded? The authors note 50% fluorescence retention at 3 d.
9. Multiple testing: >50 outcomes across figures with only within-family Holm-Sidak. Is there any correction across families?
10. Unit of analysis is the mouse and mice were singly housed (good), but 16S n=6 of 10 mice - how were the 6 selected?
11. Table 1 shows identical means/SDs for ND and ND+MP across Scd1, Elovl6 and Fasn - transcription error or real? Check Table S2.
12. Liver: absolute liver weight was LOWER in HFD+MP than HFD while steatosis was higher. Is this consistent? Was body weight identical?
13. Blinding and randomisation are not stated for group allocation, histological scoring (NAS is subjective), or flow-cytometry gating.
14. Time of sacrifice / circadian phase is not stated; lipogenic genes (Scd1, Fasn, Elovl6) are strongly diurnal, and all mice were fasted 16 h, which itself suppresses lipogenic genes.
15. Was liver RNA from the same mice as the histology, and was any batch structure (qPCR plate = group?) present?

## Relevance to the team
Closest published analogue of a PS-MP x high-fat-diet 2x2 mouse study with liver readouts: it reports hepatic lipogenic (Scd1/Elovl6/Fasn) and inflammatory (Tnfa/Il6/Il1b) induction by MP ONLY on the high-fat background, with null MP effects on normal diet - the same "exacerbation" pattern the team is testing for PE x MASH diet. Crucially, that exacerbation was never tested as a formal interaction (one-way ANOVA + pairwise only), and the readout is targeted qPCR, not transcriptome-wide, so it supports a directional prior (lipogenesis up, inflammation up) rather than a quantitative benchmark. It also offers no FAO/PPARalpha, oxidative-stress, mitochondrial or circadian data.
