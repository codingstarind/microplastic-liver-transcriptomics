# advsci2026_pet_gutliver

Advanced Science, 2026; volume 13, issue 1, article e12030. DOI 10.1002/advs.202512030. PMID 41124673. PMCID PMC12766997. Link: https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202512030

## Access level read

Abstract, Introduction, Results, all main figure legends (Figs 1-6), Discussion, full Experimental Section, Statistics, Data Availability, Conflict of Interest read. ## Biological question
Whether chronic (29-week) oral exposure to environmentally mimetic, mechanically abraded PET microplastics - as opposed to the uniform spherical polystyrene beads used in most studies - causes hepatic injury and NAFLD-like disease, and whether this involves disruption of the gut-liver axis (microbiota, mucus barrier, bile flow). "This study investigates the hepatotoxicity of the PET-MPs following long-term exposure, with a focus on the gut-liver axis interactions and transcriptomic responses" (Abstract).

## Experimental design
Two groups only: control (Con) and PET-MP (MP); two time points (8 weeks and 29 weeks of exposure). No diet challenge arm - this is a 1-factor (exposure) x 2-timepoint design on standard chow, NOT a 2x2 factorial.
"Seventeen mice were assigned to the control group and 16 mice to the PET-MP ingestion group. At 8 weeks of feeding, 8 mice from each group were sacrificed, and at 29 weeks, the remaining animals were sacrificed, resulting in 8 mice in the 8-week control group, 8 mice in the 8-week MP group, 9 mice in the 29-week control group, and 8 mice in the 29-week MP group" (Experimental Section, Mouse Husbandry).
Randomisation: stated - "they were randomly assigned to groups at the start of the experiment, based on the balanced initial body weights" (Mouse Husbandry).
Blinding: stated for histopathology only - "an experienced pathologist scored the NAFLD activity in a blinded manner according to the criteria established by the Non-alcoholic Steatohepatitis Clinical Research Network" (Methods, H&E and NAFLD Activity Scoring). No blinding statement for other readouts.
Control definition is loose: "Mice that did not ingest the PET-MPs were designated as the control (Con) group" - no vehicle/sham particle control, and the route is described as MP "incorporated into their feed", though the Results say "via oral administration"; AMBIGUOUS (see points to verify).

## Species/strain/sex/age; tissue(s)
Mouse, C57BL/6N, MALE only, obtained from Samtako Bio-Korea. "A four-week-old male C57BL/6N mice (total number of mice: 33, mean initial body weight: 18-19 g) were used"; 7-day acclimation; exposure from 5 weeks of age for 29 weeks. Note an internal inconsistency on final age: the Abstract says "from 5 to 34 weeks of age" while Methods say "The mice were raised for 29 weeks and sacrificed at 33 weeks of age"; AMBIGUOUS.
Housing: 25 +/- 2 C, 50 +/- 5% RH, 12-h light-dark cycle, "lights on from 07:00 to 19:00", chow and sterilised water ad libitum.
Tissues: liver (main), small intestine (Swiss roll, PAS), feces (16S, color), whole body (DXA), serum (ALT). Omics tissues: liver (bulk RNA-seq) and feces (16S rRNA amplicon). No adipose, muscle or brain omics.

## Biological replicates per group (per assay)
- Total cohort n = 33 (17 Con, 16 MP); terminal groups 8 / 8 / 9 / 8 as quoted above.
- Body weight and body composition: "Body weight (n = 33)" (Fig. 1B legend); "Body composition and bone-related parameters (n = 33)" (Methods).
- Liver weight: "Liver weight (n = 21) was measured immediately after sacrifice" (Fig. 3B legend) - n = 21 does not correspond to any group total; AMBIGUOUS (which animals/timepoints does this pool?).
- Serum ALT: "Blood alanine aminotransferase (ALT) (n = 13)" (Fig. 3F legend) - again unexplained; AMBIGUOUS.
- RNA-seq: "Total RNA was extracted from mouse liver samples (n = 4 per group)" (Methods, Transcriptomic Analysis). So 4 per group per time point (presumably 16 libraries; not stated explicitly).
- 16S microbiome, histology, IHC, qRT-PCR: n NOT REPORTED per assay (searched figure legends and Methods). Legends only say "Each experiment was independently repeated at least three times", which is ambiguous between technical repeats and biological n.

## Polymer; particle size; supplier; label; characterisation; endotoxin
- Polymer: polyethylene terephthalate (PET) ONLY, produced in-house by mechanical abrasion of new drinking-water bottles ("Samdasoo, Korea"). No commercial monodisperse beads; no fluorescent or other label.
- Preparation/contamination control (unusually detailed): labels/caps removed, triple rinse with 18.2 MOhm.cm water, 70% ethanol 10 min, ISO Class 5 laminar flow drying on pre-combusted aluminium foil, UV-C (254 nm) 30 min per side, cleaned stainless-steel mill and sieves, nitrile gloves and cotton coats. "High-temperature/autoclave sterilization was not used to avoid PET deformation or hydrolysis" (Methods, PET-MP Preparation).
- Size: sieved < 500 um; measured by laser diffraction (Mastersizer 3000, dry dispersion, 3 bar, triplicate). "The majority of PET-MPs were within the 10-200 um range, with approximately 60% of the particles between 10-100 um and 26% between 100-200 um" (Results). Full distribution is in Table S1 (NOT VERIFIED). These are TRUE microplastics, 2-3 orders of magnitude larger than the 50-500 nm beads used in the Nat Metab study.
- Morphology: FIB-SEM (Zeiss Crossbeam 550) - "irregular morphology and surface roughness ... heterogeneous shapes with sharp edges and a broad size distribution" (Results; Fig. S3, images NOT VERIFIED).
- Endotoxin: NOT REPORTED (no endotoxin or LPS assay on the particles; searched full text). Note the authors invoke LPS translocation as a mechanism but never measure circulating LPS - they list it as future work.
- Zeta potential / surface chemistry / leachate (e.g. antimony, phthalates, residual monomers from bottle PET): NOT REPORTED.

## Dose; route; vehicle; frequency; duration
"The experimental group (designated as 'MP' in this study) ingested PET-MP incorporated into their feed at a dose of 5 mg per week for 29 weeks" (Methods). Dose rationale quoted: "The PET-MP dose (5 mg week-1) was determined by adjusting the reported human global average rate of Microplastic intake (GARMI, 5 g week-1) based on the human-to-mouse body weight ratio."
Route: in feed (Methods) / "via oral administration" (Results) - the two statements differ; no gavage procedure is described. Vehicle: none stated (dry particles in feed). Frequency: weekly. Duration: 29 weeks, with an 8-week interim sacrifice. Approximate per-kg dose is not given by the authors; at ~25-30 g body weight, 5 mg/week is roughly 170-200 mg/kg/week - the same order as the 100 mg/kg/week in the Nat Metab study, though administered as large irregular fragments rather than nanoscale/submicron spheres.

## Diet / metabolic-stress model
NONE. Standard chow ad libitum for both groups ("The mice were provided chow and sterilized water ad libitum throughout the study"). No HFD, no MASH/NASH diet, no fructose or cholesterol. Chow brand, % kcal fat and supplier are NOT REPORTED (searched Methods). Obesity and steatosis in this study arise from the MP exposure itself on chow, not from a dietary challenge - so there is NO microplastic x diet interaction in this paper at all.

## Data types
- Bulk liver RNA-seq: total RNA (RNeasy), RINe >= 7.0, >= 200 ng input, rRNA depletion (MGI), directional library (MGI), sequenced on MGI DNBSEQ G400-RS at the KNU NGS Core Facility, paired-end. Read depth / number of reads per sample NOT REPORTED. QC: SOAPnuke, Phred >= 30, "Over 96% of the paired-end reads from all samples passed the quality filtering"; alignment HISAT2 to GRCm39; assembly StringTie2.
- 16S rRNA amplicon (V4) of feces, Illumina MiSeq v2 300-cycle, QIIME2 v2024.5, DADA2, SILVA v138, rarefied "to a depth of 1500 reads".
- Histology: H&E with NAS scoring, Oil Red O (10 um cryosections), Masson's trichrome, PAS (small intestine mucus, QuPath v0.5.1), IHC for alpha-SMA and PLIN2, immunofluorescence for CD68.
- qRT-PCR: hepatic Tnfa and Il6, 2^-ddCt with GAPDH as internal control.
- Serum ALT (no AST reported).
- DXA body composition (InAlyzer).
- Pyrolysis-GC/MS of liver targeting benzoic acid (PET marker) - result NEGATIVE.
- No proteomics, no lipidomics, no metabolomics, no single-cell work.

## Statistics & computational methods
- Phenotype/histology/qPCR: GraphPad Prism 10.5.0; "Unpaired t-tests were conducted to evaluate differences between groups at each time point"; mean +/- SEM; p < 0.05 significance. NOTE: two-group-at-each-timepoint t-tests only - NO two-way ANOVA, no exposure x time interaction test, and no multiple-testing correction mentioned for the many parallel endpoints.
- RNA-seq differential expression: the DE TOOL, NORMALISATION AND SIGNIFICANCE THRESHOLDS ARE NOT REPORTED. The Methods stop at StringTie2; no DESeq2/edgeR/limma, no fold-change or FDR cut-off, no statement of multiple-testing correction (searched the whole Experimental Section and figure legends). DEG counts are given in Results ("At 8 weeks, 13 genes were markedly downregulated, and 15 were upregulated ... By 29 weeks ... 24 downregulated and 59 upregulated genes") but the criteria behind them are unverifiable from the paper.
- GO enrichment: "Gene Ontology (GO) enrichment analysis" and a gene-concept network are shown (Fig. 4D,E) but the TOOL, background gene set and significance threshold are NOT REPORTED.
- Microbiome: R v4.0.2, vegan v2.6-4, phyloseq v1.30.0, PCoA on Bray-Curtis of ASVs, PERMANOVA (Adonis). Reported: "At 8 weeks, the MP group demonstrated significant separation (Adonis R2 = 0.176, p = 0.006) ... By week 29, the separation persisted, although the effect size was slightly decreased (Adonis R2 = 0.114, p = 0.028)" (Results). Differential genus abundance method and correction NOT REPORTED (volcano plots in Fig. 2C).

## Main findings with effect directions and statistical evidence
Body composition
- INCREASED body weight in MP at 29 weeks, no difference at 8 weeks: "By week 29, mice in the MP group had substantially higher body weights than those in the Con group (Figure 1B). During the 8-week study period, no considerable differences were observed" (Results). VALIDATED (weights, unpaired t-test).
- INCREASED fat mass by DXA with unchanged lean mass (Fig. 1D,E). VALIDATED (DXA). Note the sentence as published is garbled - "revealing no change in lean mass or an increase in fat mass in the MP group" - direction is taken from the Abstract ("increased adiposity and obesity") and the figure description.

Gut
- Gut microbiota composition DIFFERS by exposure at both time points (PERMANOVA values above). VALIDATED (16S + PERMANOVA) as a community-level difference; genus-level calls are OMICS-ONLY.
- Firmicutes UP, Bacteroidota DOWN at both time points (Fig. 2B, donut plots, described qualitatively - no test reported). OMICS-ONLY.
- Bacteroides DOWN and several Lachnospiraceae genera DOWN in MP (Fig. 2C). OMICS-ONLY (differential-abundance method not stated). SCFA/butyrate production is INFERRED from taxonomy - no SCFA was measured.
- DECREASED intestinal mucus: reduced PAS-positive area in small intestine, "more pronounced at 29 weeks" (Fig. 2D-F). VALIDATED (histology + QuPath quantification).
- Fecal color brightening (Fig. S1) interpreted as altered bile secretion/flow. The authors themselves frame this as a hypothesis: "This raises the hypothesis that MP ingestion influences the hepatobiliary-pancreatic axis". OBSERVATIONAL/qualitative - no bile acid measurement of any kind was performed, despite the Abstract's claim of "Disruption of bile acid metabolism"; treat that abstract claim as NOT directly supported by measured data.

Liver phenotype at 29 weeks
- Gross steatotic appearance; "Quantitative liver weight measurements revealed no statistically significant differences between the groups" (Fig. 3B) - note this CONTRADICTS the Abstract's claim that "mice exposed to PET-MPs developed hepatomegaly". Flag this discrepancy.
- NAFLD-like histology: ballooning, lipid accumulation, inflammatory infiltration, Mallory-Denk bodies; higher NAFLD activity score in MP (Fig. 3C-E). VALIDATED (blinded NAS scoring by a pathologist).
- INCREASED serum ALT at 29 weeks only (Fig. 3F). VALIDATED (serum biochemistry). AST not measured.
- INCREASED CD68+ macrophages by immunofluorescence (Fig. 3G; Fig. S2A). VALIDATED (IF).
- INCREASED hepatic Tnfa and Il6 mRNA at 29 weeks (Fig. 3H,I; Fig. S2B,C). VALIDATED (qRT-PCR). NF-kB, NLRP3, IL-1beta were NOT measured; NF-kB appears only in the Introduction/Discussion as literature.
- INCREASED hepatic lipid droplets by Oil Red O, maximal at 29 weeks (Fig. 5A,B). VALIDATED (histology + ImageJ).
- INCREASED PLIN2 protein by IHC (Fig. 5C). VALIDATED (immunohistochemistry) - this is the one lipid-droplet transcript that is confirmed at protein level.
- Early fibrosis at 29 weeks: alpha-SMA+ signal (IHC) and increased collagen-positive area by Masson's trichrome (Fig. 6A-C). VALIDATED (histology/IHC). Absent at 8 weeks.

Liver transcriptomics (bulk RNA-seq, n = 4/group)
- Small DEG numbers: 8 weeks 13 down / 15 up; 29 weeks 24 down / 59 up (Results; Fig. 4B,C). OMICS-ONLY, and the thresholds are unstated so these counts cannot be reproduced.
- Named genes: Poli, Col12a1, Mki67 at 8 weeks; Apoa4 and Mthfd1l among upregulated at 29 weeks (Results). OMICS-ONLY; none of these was confirmed by qPCR or protein (the only confirmed targets are Tnfa/Il6 by qPCR and PLIN2 by IHC).
- GO enrichment at 29 weeks: "substantial enrichment observed in pathways associated with lipid catabolism, fatty acid biosynthesis, and steroid metabolism" plus long-chain fatty acid metabolism, olefinic compound metabolism and "cellular responses to xenobiotic stimuli" (Results; Fig. 4D,E). OMICS-ONLY. Note lipid CATABOLISM and fatty acid BIOSYNTHESIS are reported as enriched together, and the enrichment direction (up or down) is not resolved in the readable text.
- Lipid-droplet gene set UP: Chka, Plin2, Ces1c at 8 weeks; a broader LD gene panel up at 29 weeks (Fig. 5D,E). OMICS-ONLY except Plin2 (PLIN2 protein VALIDATED by IHC).
- Collagen/ECM genes: NO change at 8 weeks, UP at 29 weeks (Fig. 6D,E) - concordant with the MT/alpha-SMA histology, so the fibrosis direction is corroborated by an independent assay even though the individual genes are omics-only.
- Mitochondrial energy metabolism genes DOWN (Abstract: "the downregulation of genes involved in mitochondrial energy metabolism"). OMICS-ONLY - I could NOT locate a Results paragraph, figure panel or gene list supporting this claim in the readable main text, and NO mitochondrial functional assay (respiration, membrane potential, mtDNA) was performed. Treat as an abstract-level claim pending Supporting Information.
- NO MP x diet interaction and no interaction test of any kind; even exposure x time was analysed only as separate t-tests at each time point.

Exposure/biodistribution
- PET was NOT detected in liver by pyrolysis-GC/MS: "PET was not detected in liver tissues of the CON and MP groups by Py-GC/MS analysis targeting benzoic acid (Table S3)" (Results). This is important: the hepatic phenotype is NOT accompanied by demonstrated hepatic particle burden, and the authors concede "definitive evidence of direct particle accumulation in the liver remains to be established" (Discussion).

## Pathway-theme directions
- Lipid droplet / lipid storage: UP (Plin2, Chka, Ces1c and a wider LD panel) - OMICS-ONLY, with PLIN2 protein VALIDATED and ORO histology VALIDATED.
- Lipogenesis: "fatty acid biosynthesis" among enriched GO terms at 29 weeks - OMICS-ONLY, direction of the term not explicitly stated. Canonical genes Fasn, Srebf1, Acly, Pparg, Cd36 are NOT named anywhere in the readable text.
- PPAR/FAO: PPAR signalling is discussed only as prior literature ("MP exposure changes the pathways involved in PPAR signaling, oxidative stress, and complement and coagulation cascades" - Discussion, citing others). Ppara, Cpt1a, Acox1, Cyp4a are NOT reported in this study's own data. "Lipid catabolism" appears as an enriched GO term, direction unresolved. No FAO measurement.
- Cholesterol / steroid: "steroid metabolism" enriched at 29 weeks - OMICS-ONLY. No serum lipid panel (no cholesterol, HDL or triglycerides measured).
- Inflammation: UP - hepatic Tnfa and Il6 mRNA increased (VALIDATED, qPCR), CD68+ macrophages increased (VALIDATED, IF), lobular inflammation scored in NAS (VALIDATED, blinded histology). NF-kB, NLRP3, IL-1beta: not measured. LPS/endotoxemia: proposed mechanism, NOT measured (explicitly deferred to future work).
- Oxidative stress: NOT MEASURED in this study. ROS, MDA, GSH, SOD, Nrf2 and Hmox1 appear only as literature in the Introduction/Discussion; the authors state "We will address this in future studies through targeted assays for oxidative stress markers (MDA, GSH/GSSG), circulating LPS, and ferroptosis-related proteins (GPX4, ACSL4)" (Discussion).
- Mitochondria: DOWN at transcript level per the Abstract only - OMICS-ONLY and not locatable in the main text; no functional mitochondrial assay.
- Fibrosis: UP at 29 weeks (alpha-SMA IHC, MT collagen area, collagen/ECM transcripts) and absent at 8 weeks. VALIDATED (histology) + concordant OMICS.
- Circadian: NOT addressed. No clock genes. Lighting is given (lights on 07:00-19:00) but the time of sacrifice is NOT reported.
- Interaction (MP x diet): not applicable - there is no diet factor. Exposure x time was never tested formally.

## Author-acknowledged limitations (quotes + location)
- Mechanism: "A key limitation of this study is the lack of detailed mechanistic exploration at the cellular level. While our in vivo model allowed us to assess the chronic systemic effects of PET-MPs, it does not fully clarify how PET-MPs initiate hepatocellular injury and metabolic disruption" (Discussion).
- Unmeasured mediators: "We will address this in future studies through targeted assays for oxidative stress markers (MDA, GSH/GSSG), circulating LPS, and ferroptosis-related proteins (GPX4, ACSL4)" (Discussion).
- Sex and strain: "our use of male C57BL/6N mice improved internal validity and comparability with existing hepatology/metabolism datasets but limits generalizability due to strain-specific immune/metabolic features and the exclusion of females" (Discussion).
- No toxicokinetics / no tissue burden: "This investigation was designed as a hazard-focused study rather than a pharmacokinetic/toxicokinetic analysis; polymer-specific in-tissue detection, biodistribution, and kinetic profiling were not performed. PET was not detected in the liver tissues of the MP group by Pyro-GC/MS analysis" and "we interpret our findings as hepatic alterations associated with prolonged PET-MP exposure, while acknowledging that definitive evidence of direct particle accumulation in the liver remains to be established" (Discussion).
- Excretion: "without polymer-specific quantification or degradation product analysis, excretion kinetics cannot be inferred" (Discussion).
- Fecal color confound acknowledged: "Although different factors, including diet and microbial metabolism, can influence fecal color..." (Results).

## Methodological points to verify independently
(Questions, not accusations.)
1. Internal inconsistency on age: Abstract "from 5 to 34 weeks of age" vs Methods "sacrificed at 33 weeks of age" after 29 weeks of exposure starting at ~5 weeks. Which is right?
2. Hepatomegaly: the Abstract says the mice "developed hepatomegaly", but Fig. 3B legend and Results say liver weight did not differ significantly. Which claim does the data support?
3. "Disruption of bile acid metabolism" (Abstract) - was any bile acid actually measured, or does this rest entirely on fecal colour and Figure S1? Check Supporting Information.
4. "Downregulation of genes involved in mitochondrial energy metabolism" (Abstract) - find the supporting panel/gene list; it is not in the main-text Results.
5. RNA-seq DE method, normalisation, fold-change cut-off, p/FDR threshold and multiple-testing correction are not stated anywhere. With n = 4/group and 13-59 DEGs, were these raw-p DEGs or FDR-corrected? This is essential before comparing DEG counts with the team's own.
6. Sequencing depth per library, number of libraries, batch/lane structure and whether the 8-week and 29-week samples were sequenced together are all unreported - is time point confounded with sequencing batch?
7. GO enrichment tool, gene universe and threshold are unreported; was the background the detected transcriptome or all genes?
8. Route ambiguity: "incorporated into their feed" (Methods) vs "via oral administration" (Results). If in feed, how was per-mouse intake of 5 mg/week ensured, and were mice singly or group housed? If group housed, the cage - not the mouse - may be the unit for intake and for microbiome.
9. Cage/litter structure is not reported at all, which matters most for the 16S data (cage effects dominate mouse microbiome studies) and for a feed-delivered exposure.
10. Control group received no sham/vehicle and no particle-free equivalent handling ("Mice that did not ingest the PET-MPs were designated as the control"). Were control mice handled identically?
11. n values in Fig. 3B (n = 21) and Fig. 3F (n = 13) do not match any stated group or total. Which animals and which time points do these pool, and were any excluded?
12. "Each experiment was independently repeated at least three times" in every figure legend - does this mean three independent animal cohorts (there was only one cohort of 33) or technical replication? Likely technical; worth confirming before treating it as biological replication.
13. Multiple comparisons: dozens of endpoints each tested by unpaired t-test at two time points, with no correction. How many of the single-asterisk findings would survive correction?
14. Microbiome rarefaction to 1500 reads per sample is shallow by current standards; how many samples were dropped, and what was the per-group n for 16S?
15. Differential-abundance method for the genus-level volcano plots (Fig. 2C) is unreported - was any FDR control applied?
16. Particle leachates: bottle-derived PET can carry antimony catalyst residues, oligomers and additives. Was any leachate/chemical characterisation or endotoxin test performed on the ground material?
17. Chow composition (fat %, supplier) is not reported, yet the control mice are the reference for "obesity" - what was the background diet?
18. Time of sacrifice/circadian phase is not reported; hepatic lipid and bile-acid gene expression is strongly diurnal.
19. The Py-GC/MS liver result was negative for PET - was the method's limit of detection and a spike-recovery control established (the authors list spike-recovery only as future work)?

## Relevance to the team
A chronic (29-week) bulk liver RNA-seq study in male C57BL/6N mice with a different polymer (PET), a very different particle size class (10-200 um irregular fragments vs 10-50 um nominal PE), and no diet factor - so it offers a direction-of-effect comparison (steatosis, LD genes up, collagen/ECM up, Tnfa/Il6 up, fibrosis late) rather than a design parallel to the PE x MASH 2x2. Its DEG counts are small (13-59) and its DE thresholds, sequencing depth and batch structure are unreported, and the data are not deposited, so none of its transcriptomic numbers can be independently re-derived. Its hepatic phenotype is validated by blinded histology, ALT, IHC and qPCR, while mitochondrial, bile-acid, oxidative-stress and LPS mechanisms are claimed but not measured.
