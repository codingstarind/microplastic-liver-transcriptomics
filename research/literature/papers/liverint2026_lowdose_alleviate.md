## Main findings with effect directions and statistical evidence
All of the following are from the Abstract (Results/Conclusion); none could be checked against figures, legends or methods.
- CONTRASTING HEADLINE RESULT - ALLEVIATION, not exacerbation: "H&E staining revealed significant lipid accumulation, inflammation, and hepatic fibrosis in livers from HFD group, whereas no obvious pathological changes were observed in NP group. Notably, these effects were greatly diminished in HFD-NP group, compared with HFD group." Directions: steatosis DOWN, inflammation DOWN, fibrosis DOWN when low-dose PS-NPs are added to HFD. VALIDATED (histology, H&E) as far as the abstract states; no quantification given.
- PS-NPs alone were inert at this dose: "no obvious pathological changes were observed in NP group" - matches the chow-diet null effect reported by both exacerbation papers, so the two literatures agree on the particle-alone arm and disagree only on the combined arm.
- In vitro: "PS-NPs displayed no apparent effect on the viability of primary hepatocytes, while significantly alleviated palmitic acid (PA)-induced hepatocyte apoptosis and lipid droplet accumulation." Directions: apoptosis DOWN, lipid droplets DOWN. VALIDATED (cell viability, apoptosis and lipid-droplet assays - specific assays not named).
- Lipid handling: "hepatic lipid oxidation and utilization were enhanced in HFD-NP rats, suggesting that PS-NPs may modulate lipid metabolism upon HFD." Direction: fatty-acid oxidation UP. VALIDATED as a measurement in principle, assay NOT VERIFIED.
- Mechanism (transcriptome-derived): "Transcriptomic analysis revealed the mechanism might involve the activated AMPK signalling pathway and inhibited mTOR signalling pathway, important regulators for autophagy, implying the involvement of lipophagy in this process." Directions: AMPK UP, mTOR DOWN. OMICS-ONLY at this step (note the hedging "might involve", "implying").
- Causal test: "in vitro experiments showed the inhibition of lipid droplet autophagy exacerbated HFD-induced hepatotoxicity." VALIDATED by pharmacological inhibition (autophagy inhibitor, and per Methods an AMPK manipulation) - in vitro only; the abstract reports no in vivo autophagy/AMPK blockade.
- Conclusion verbatim: "low-dose PS-NPs can activate the AMPK pathway and lipophagy, thereby alleviating liver injury through a stress adaptation response under high-fat diet conditions. Our findings elucidate the context-dependent interaction between microplastics and dietary patterns in liver diseases development".

## Which doses/sizes/conditions gave alleviation vs harm (as requested)
ALLEVIATION was reported under, and only under, this combination: polystyrene nanoparticles, dose described only as "low-dose" (NO NUMBER GIVEN - NOT VERIFIED), particle size NOT REPORTED, in MALE WISTAR RATS, co-administered with a high-fat diet for 90 days; and in vitro in rat primary hepatocytes co-treated with PS-NPs and palmitic acid for 24 h. Endpoints alleviated: hepatic lipid accumulation, inflammation, fibrosis (H&E), hepatocyte apoptosis and lipid droplets. Proposed mediator: AMPK activation / mTOR inhibition driving lipophagy.
NO HARM was reported anywhere in the abstract at this dose: the PS-NP-only arm showed "no obvious pathological changes", and there was "no apparent effect on the viability of primary hepatocytes". The only "harm" direction in the abstract is the pathology of the HFD group itself, and the exacerbation produced by BLOCKING autophagy in vitro - not by the particles.
The authors attribute the contrast with the exacerbation literature to DOSE ("current studies predominantly focus on the hepatotoxicity of high-dose MPs") and to context ("context-dependent interaction between microplastics and dietary patterns"). Because the numeric dose, the particle size and the control-group structure are all unavailable from the abstract, the dose at which the direction flips CANNOT be established from this record. Anyone citing this as a dose-response contrast must obtain the full text first.

## Methodological points to verify independently
(All of these require the full text; they are listed so the team knows exactly what to extract if they obtain it.)
1. The numeric dose, and the units, of "low-dose" PS-NPs - and how it compares with the doses in the exacerbation studies (e.g. 1 mg/L drinking water / 0.18 ug/g BW/day in liverint2025_np_masld; 0.5 mg/animal/day in ijbs2025_psnp_lysosome).
2. Particle size, supplier, surface charge/functionalisation, characterisation (DLS/zeta/TEM), aggregation and endotoxin.
3. The actual group structure: are there four groups (control, NP, HFD, HFD-NP)? Is there a normal-diet control? The abstract names two groups in Methods but a third ("NP group") in Results.
4. n per group, and whether the transcriptomics n matches the phenotype n.
5. SPECIES DIFFERENCE: this is the only one of the three papers in Wistar RATS rather than C57BL/6 mice - rat/mouse differences in lipid handling and in plastic-particle uptake should be weighed before pooling directions across these papers.
6. Route of administration (gavage vs drinking water vs diet admixture) and vehicle - route strongly affects delivered dose and GI exposure.
7. HFD formulation, % kcal fat, supplier and the control diet; 90 days in rats is a different exposure window from 12-20 weeks in mice.
8. Whether histological scoring (steatosis, inflammation, fibrosis) was blinded and semi-quantitative, or only representative images. "Fibrosis" from H&E alone would need a collagen stain to confirm.
9. Transcriptomics: platform, tissue, n, DE tool, thresholds, multiple-testing correction, and whether AMPK/mTOR enrichment survives correction; whether an interaction contrast was run.
10. Whether the in vitro AMPK manipulation was inhibition, activation or knockdown, and whether it rescued/abolished the protective effect (the abstract only reports the autophagy-inhibition arm clearly).
11. Whether any hormetic/biphasic dose-response was tested, or only a single low dose - this determines whether "low-dose protective, high-dose harmful" is actually supported or merely asserted by cross-study comparison.

## Relevance to the team
This is the direct counter-example to the two exacerbation papers: same general exposure (oral polystyrene nanoplastics + a high-fat diet, 90 days) but the opposite direction - steatosis, inflammation and fibrosis all REDUCED in the plastic-plus-HFD group, with FAO up, AMPK up, mTOR down and lipophagy proposed as the mechanism (the inverse of the mTOR-up/autophagy-blocked mechanism in ijbs2025_psnp_lysosome). The three papers therefore differ in direction while sharing the finding that plastic alone on a control diet does little, which makes "plastic x diet" an interaction question rather than a main-effect question - precisely the contrast the team's 2x2 re-analysis must test formally rather than by pairwise comparison. The usable caveat is that the dose, size, route, species (rat, not mouse) and n behind this reversal are all unverifiable from the abstract, so the paper should be cited as a direction-discrepant report pending full-text extraction, not as evidence for a specific protective dose.
