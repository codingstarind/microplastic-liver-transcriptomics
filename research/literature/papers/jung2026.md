## Biological question
How does polyethylene (PE) microplastic — "the most prevalent yet relatively understudied plastic type" — alter liver gene expression, cell composition and zonation, alone and on a MASH-inducing diet, and which nuclear-receptor (NR) programs mediate it? (Abstract; Introduction last two paragraphs.)

## Experimental design
- **In vitro polymer screen (first experiment):** mouse primary hepatocytes, 24 h, seven MP types compared for viability (MTT). "we initially tested various types of MPs including PE, PS, PET, PP, polyvinylidene chloride (PVDC), polyamide-6 (PA-6), and polyvinyl chloride (PVC) in primary hepatocytes after 24 hours of exposure and found that PE resulted in the lowest cell viability (fig. S1A)" (Results, first paragraph). Raw MTT values stated to be in table S1 (not available to me). No per-polymer n, no statistics for the screen, and no viability percentages are given in the main text — **not reported** in the readable text.
- **In vivo: 2 × 2 factorial.** Factors = diet (CD vs MD) × gavage (Veh vs PE); groups CD+Veh, CD+PE, MD+Veh, MD+PE. Reference group for most comparisons is CD+Veh; MD+Veh is the within-diet control for PE.
- **Randomisation:** not reported (Methods "Animals and in vivo treatments" contains no randomisation statement).
- **Blinding:** only for histology — "Histopathological analysis, including nonalcoholic fatty liver disease activity scoring, was performed in a blinded manner" (Methods, Histology). No blinding stated for any other readout.
- **Housing:** "Groups of mice (n = 4 or 5 per group) were housed together" (Methods) — i.e. cage is co-extensive with treatment group; cage is not used as a statistical unit anywhere.
- **No sham (non-gavaged) arm and no inert/non-plastic particle control** are described anywhere in Methods or figure legends.

## Species/strain/sex/age; tissue(s)
- In vivo: "Eight-week-old male C57BL/6 were procured from the Jackson Laboratory" (Methods, Animals). Male only; no female cohort. Housing 22 ± 1 °C, 25 ± 5% humidity, 12-h light/dark, food and water ad libitum.
- In vitro: "Mouse primary hepatocytes were isolated from male C57BL/6 WT male mice using the collagenase perfusion methods" (Methods, Cell culture).
- Tissues: liver (bulk RNA-seq, FFPE sections for Xenium/H&E/Raman/O-PTIR, hepatic TG, ChIP-qPCR, RT-qPCR); serum (ALT); blood (glucose); body and liver weight.
- Ethics: IACUC, University of Oklahoma Health Sciences Center, protocol 23-056-SEAFHI.

## Dose; route; vehicle; frequency; duration
- "2 mg PE ... suspended in 0.1% Tween 20 + 99.9% molecular-grade water was administered by oral gavage per mouse per day for 8 weeks" (Methods, Animals). Dose is **per mouse per day**, not per kg; no body-weight normalisation is reported.
- Vehicle groups: "mice were orally gavaged daily with 0.1% Tween 20 of 99.9% molecular-grade water without PE" (Methods, Animals).
- Duration 8 weeks for both diet and gavage; "Body weight and food intake were measured weekly" (food-intake data are not shown in the readable figures/legends). Euthanasia by isoflurane overdose.

## Statistics & computational methods
- **Bulk DE:** Salmon v1.10.3 selective alignment (--validateMapping, -l A, --seqBias, --useVBOpt) against GENCODE GRCm39; fastp v0.23.4 QC; tximport; DESeq2 with estimateSizeFactors then DESeq. Thresholds: "a log2 fold change (FC) cutoff of +/-0.38 and a false discovery rate (FDR) threshold of less than 0.05" (Methods, Bulk RNA-seq). Comparisons are **pairwise condition contrasts**; no diet x PE interaction term is described anywhere in Methods, Results or legends.
- **GSEA:** fgsea in R, ranked gene statistics, gene-set size min 1 / max 500, 10,000 permutations; custom module "LIPID_METABOLISM_AND_TOXICITY". Min gene-set size of 1 is unusually permissive.
- **PCA:** DESeq2 plotPCA; heatmaps pheatmap on log2(mean+1), row-scaled.
- **Xenium processing:** Seurat v5.2.0; QC filter "low-quality cells with less than 25 unique transcripts"; SCTransform normalisation; Harmony batch correction "across experimental groups and samples"; UMAP (50 neighbours, min.dist 0.001, spread 1); FindClusters resolution 0.8 -> 32 clusters, manually annotated against a reference liver atlas into 15 cell types; mixed-lineage cells excluded.
- **Xenium DE:** FindMarkers/pairwise DESeq2 depending on analysis; thresholds |log2FC| >= 0.38, minimum expressing-cell proportion 0.33, FDR < 0.05 (Fig. 4B legend and Methods); for the spatial-inflammation DEGs, |log2FC| >= 0.58, min proportion 0.25, FDR < 0.05.
- **Pseudo-bulk / replicate handling (important):** several analyses explicitly aggregate to the mouse: "spatial data were pseudo-bulked by summing raw gene expression counts across all segmented cells within each spatial capture area"; "Gene expression was aggregated using the AggregateExpression function, followed by normalization with DESeq2" for GSEA; and for diversity, "To avoid pseudoreplication, cluster-level indices were averaged to the biological sample level, and the mean Shannon index per sample was used for downstream analysis" (Methods, Sample-based analysis). Fig. 4E boxplots are "sample-level aggregated and normalized counts ... Pairwise differential expression was performed via DESeq2".
  However, other spatial comparisons (FindMarkers across cells, Spearman correlation of Anxa2 with other genes "Normalized expression data from SCT assay were extracted, and gene-wise Spearman correlations were computed") operate at the **cell level**, where each mouse contributes thousands of cells - these are not stated to be corrected for sample as a random effect.
- **Cell-proportion statistics:** beta regression (betareg) on sample-level proportions with emmeans and Holm-Bonferroni adjustment; cluster-level proportions by beta GLMM (glmmTMB) with "biological sample as a random intercept [Proportion ~ condition + (1 | sample)]"; cluster-level Shannon by linear mixed model (lme4) "[Shannon ~ condition + (1 | sample)]" with Tukey adjustment. This is the one place where mouse is modelled as a random effect.
- **Phenotype statistics (Fig. 1):** "Statistical significance was determined using ordinary one-way analysis of variance (ANOVA) with Tukey's multiple comparisons test" (Fig. 1 legend) - i.e. the 2 x 2 design is analysed as four independent groups by one-way ANOVA, **not** as a two-way ANOVA with an interaction term. Same approach in the general Methods statistics section and for Fig. 6 ("ordinary one-way ANOVA with Tukey's multiple comparisons test or Spearman correlation analysis").
- **Raman band statistics:** "Band enrichment (1454 and 1470 cm-1) was evaluated using the Mann-Whitney nonparametric test" (Fig. 5L legend).
- **Inflammation vs diversity:** Pearson correlation; "Significance boxplot comparisons were assessed using linear regression followed by Tukey's post hoc correction" (Fig. 5E legend).
- **Inflammation stratification:** spatial clusters split into inflammation-basal/middle/high "using the 0.33 and 0.67 quantiles as thresholds" across all clusters from all conditions.
- **WGCNA:** see its own bullet in Main findings.
- **Software for phenotype stats:** GraphPad Prism.

## Main findings with effect directions and statistical evidence
**Phenotype (Fig. 1; one-way ANOVA + Tukey; n stated as 5/group in Fig. 1B legend)**
- In vitro screen: PE gave "the lowest cell viability" of seven polymers (Results para 1; fig. S1A - not read). VALIDATED (MTT) but n/stats not readable.
- Body weight: CD+PE rose, "significant difference observed at the 7-week time point", sustained; no further rise with PE on MD (Results; Fig. 1B). VALIDATED (phenotype).
- Liver weight: "administration of PE did not lead to an increase in liver weight in either CD or MD groups" (Fig. 1C, fig. S1B). VALIDATED (null).
- Glucose: weight changes "were independent of glucose level" (Fig. 1D).
- ALT: "PE administration increased hepatic injury as measured by serum ALT levels in both CD- and MD-fed groups" (Fig. 1E). VALIDATED (biochemistry).
- Liver TG: "MD + PE group showed elevated TG levels compared to the MD group" (Fig. 1F). VALIDATED.
- Histology: "increased hepatic steatosis in PE-treated groups" (Fig. 1G); ballooning and inflammation only "an increasing trend ... compared to the CD-only cohort" (Fig. 1H). VALIDATED (blinded scoring); inflammation/ballooning not stated as significant.
- Authors' summary: PE for 8 weeks "results in hepatotoxicity and exacerbates diet-induced steatohepatitis". The "exacerbates" claim rests on pairwise comparisons, not an interaction test.

**Bulk RNA-seq (Fig. 2; DESeq2, |log2FC| >= 0.38, FDR < 0.05) - OMICS-ONLY**
- DEG counts: "600 and 1437 genes uniquely altered by PE and MD treatment"; "279 genes ... MD + PE group compared to the MD group"; PE-specific "503 genes uniquely altered by PE in the CD group, 182 genes in the MD group, and 97 genes shared" (Results; Fig. 2B-C; tables S5-S7).
- CD-specific PE: down Angptl8, Acp3, Lepr, Hmgcr; up Cyp4a12a, Cyp26b1, Gadd45g, Prkcb (fig. S2A-B).
- Shared PE: up Plin4, mt-Nd6, Cyb5a, Fubp3; down Pnpla3, Acly, Me1, Fasn (Fig. 2D).
- MD+PE combined: cholesterol genes Tm7sf2, Ldlr, Mylip; Pask, Oit3; inflammation Nkap (Fig. 2E) - directions not stated in text.
- Ppara "induced in CD + PE or MD + PE groups relative to CD + Veh"; Nr1i3 "induced in MD + PE groups relative to CD + Veh" (Fig. 2F-G legend). Note both are versus CD+Veh, not versus the diet-matched vehicle.
- GSEA NR lipid-metabolism/toxicity set enriched for PE in CD and MD; MD+PE vs CD+Veh "the most pronounced" (Fig. 2H-J). NES/FDR values not in text.
- GSE245069 (PS) comparison: "up-regulation of Ppara and down-regulation of Esr" in both (fig. S2F-G). OMICS-ONLY, cross-study.

**Xenium (Figs 3-5) - OMICS-ONLY unless noted**
- 15 cell types; "notable increase in the proportion of midlobular hepatocytes was observed in the PE-treated groups with a beta regression test" (Fig. 3D, fig. S3A).
- Pseudo-bulk vs bulk "strong positive correlation" (Pearson; Fig. 4A) - coefficient not in text.
- More DEGs in MD than CD groups (Fig. 4B); MD-specific PE genes include Me1, Anxa2, Ppara (Fig. 4C).
- "Ppara and Nr1i3 expression was markedly up-regulated in Kupffer cells specifically in the MD + PE condition" (Fig. 4E).
- Hepatocyte GSEA: enrichment with PE "regardless of diet; however, no significant difference was seen between the CD- and MD-fed groups" (Fig. 4I).
- Inflammation signature (Alox5ap, C3, Dnase1l3, F3, Hp, Nupr1, Reg3g, Ccr7, Il6) inversely correlated with Shannon diversity, "R = -0.52" in MD+PE (Fig. 5E).
- "Kupffer and stellate cells and lymphocytes, significantly decreased in MD + PE-treated mice compared to CD + Veh" (beta regression, Holm; Fig. 5F).
- Kupffer cells: 28 MD+PE-specific DEGs linked to "lipid-induced apoptotic cell engulfment" (Fig. 5G); central hepatocytes: 10 MD+PE-specific genes, Anxa2 "uniquely enriched in the high-inflammation regions" (Fig. 5H).

**PE detection in liver tissue (Fig. 5I-L, fig. S5K-L) - VALIDATED by spectroscopy, with limited sampling**
- Micro-Raman was run on the slides that had already been through Xenium: "Raman analysis identified PE particles on the existing Xenium slide from MD + PE samples but not in MD + Veh controls" (Results, Fig. 5 paragraph). Fig. 5I-J legend calls them "PE-like particles". The only comparison group named is MD+Veh; CD+Veh and CD+PE sections are not reported as examined. Number of particles, sections and mice: not reported.
- Co-registration with Xenium placed PE-positive spots "near Kupffer cell-enriched area (Clec4f and Folr2)" in regions III and IV (Fig. 5I-J legend).
- O-PTIR on separate serial sections "independently confirmed the PE presence by detecting its characteristic chemical signatures within the MD + PE tissues" (Results). Identification criterion: elevated 1470/1656 cm-1 ratio plus the CH2 deformation feature at ~1470-1460 cm-1, "confirmed by comparison with a PE reference spectrum acquired under identical experimental conditions" (Methods, O-PTIR). Sampling: "two independent tissue sections, with four to seven regions analyzed per section".
- Statistics for band enrichment: Mann-Whitney (Fig. 5L legend).
- Controls present: vehicle-tissue negative control (Raman, MD+Veh only), PE reference spectrum, amide I tissue reference band, exclusion of poor signal-to-noise spectra, non-plastic handling tools. Controls absent/not reported: procedural or air blanks, blank slide/paraffin controls, quantification of particle burden per gram of tissue, blinding of spectroscopists, and any CD-group tissue.

**Ppara-Anxa2 axis (Fig. 6) - the mechanistic core**
- Anxa2 higher in central hepatocytes of MD+PE vs MD+Veh (Fig. 6C); modest for CD+PE vs CD+Veh (fig. S6A). OMICS (Xenium).
- RT-qPCR on liver: "we validated significantly increased Anxa2 expression in the MD + PE group by quantitative reverse transcription polymerase chain reaction RT-qPCR (fig. S6B)". VALIDATED (qPCR, 4-5 samples).
- Spearman correlation of Anxa2 with all other panel genes in central hepatocytes, thresholded at the maximum R in the MD-effect comparison (R > 0.273): "A total of 36 MD + PE condition-specific genes correlated with Anxa2"; Enrichr/WikiPathways top term = PPAR signalling (Fig. 6E-F). COMPUTATIONAL.
- WGCNA: pseudo-bulk per biological sample (AverageExpression) on central hepatocytes only; goodSamplesGenes filter; signed network, soft power 8 chosen for signed R2 >= 0.85; blockwiseModules(minModuleSize = 15, mergeCutHeight = 0.25); module eigengene = first PC; groups encoded as binary traits; Pearson module-trait correlation with Student asymptotic p. Result: "a distinct gene module (Blue module) significantly correlated with the MD + PE condition (R = 0.68, P = 0.001)" with Ppara as "a central hub gene" and PPAR signalling the dominant pathway (fig. S6D-E). COMPUTATIONAL. Note the module-trait correlation is computed on ~19 pseudo-bulk samples with no multiple-testing correction stated across modules/traits.
- ChIP-seq (re-used public data, not generated here): "Previous raw sequence reads were uniformly processed from SRX5142528 and SRX5028180 (58)" (Methods, ChIP-seq). Reference 58 = Liang et al., Nat. Commun. 10, 1684 (2019), hepatocyte-specific GPS2 loss / PPARalpha study. Processing: SRA Toolkit, Bowtie2 to mm10, SAMtools duplicate removal, bigwig, IGV visualisation. Finding: PPARalpha "complex potentially binds to Anxa2 enhancer and promoter regions" (Fig. 6G). COMPUTATIONAL (peak visualisation; no de-novo peak calling tool named).
- ChIP-qPCR in the study's own livers: anti-PPARalpha (Invitrogen PA5-85125); "We confirmed significant PPARalpha enrichment at the selected Anxa2 peaks (p1, p2, and p3)" (Results; Fig. 6H; primers table S3). VALIDATED (ChIP-qPCR). IgG/negative-region control not named in the readable Methods.
- Agonists in vitro (mouse primary hepatocytes, 10 uM, 24 h): GW7647 and WY14643 both raised Acox1 (positive control, fig. S6F) and both raised Anxa2, "with WY14643 producing a more pronounced up-regulation than GW7647" (Fig. 6I); "cotreatment with PE further enhanced Anxa2 expression". VALIDATED (pharmacology in vitro).
- Agonist in vivo, public data: GSE282998 (B6129SF2/J mice, GW7647 at 0, 5, 10, 20 mg) - "GW7647, increased Anxa2 expression in a dose-dependent manner" (Fig. 6J). OMICS re-analysis (DESeq2).
- Knockout evidence, public data: GSE8295 microarray, "WT and Ppara-null 129S1/SvImJ mice, which were treated with either vehicle or WY14643 administered as a 0.1% CD supplement", limma with probe 1419091_a_at. WY14643 raised Anxa2 in WT (Fig. 6K) but "failed to induce any significant change in Anxa2 levels" in Ppara-null mice (Fig. 6L). VALIDATED genetically, but in a **published external dataset on a different strain background**, not in the authors' own PE-exposed mice; no Ppara-null mouse was generated or exposed to PE in this study.
- Loss of function in vitro under PE: PPARalpha antagonist GW6471 (10 uM) and Ppara siRNA (ON-TARGETplus SMARTpool L-040740-01, vs non-targeting D-001810-10) "abolished PE-induced Anxa2 up-regulation" (Fig. 6M-N). VALIDATED (pharmacological + genetic knockdown, in vitro only). Knockdown efficiency for Ppara is not reported in the readable text.
- Scope limit worth noting: the entire Ppara-dependence chain is in vitro or in external datasets; **no in vivo PE x Ppara-loss experiment** was performed.

## Pathway-theme directions
- **Lipid metabolism / lipogenesis:** mixed and partly *down* with PE. De-novo lipogenesis genes Fasn, Acly, Me1 and Pnpla3 down with PE (shared CD/MD DEGs, Fig. 2D), while Plin4 (lipid-droplet) up; liver TG up in MD+PE (Fig. 1F) and steatosis score up in PE groups (Fig. 1G). So the histological lipid phenotype increases while lipogenic transcripts decrease.
- **PPARalpha / fatty-acid oxidation:** *up*. Ppara induced in CD+PE and MD+PE vs CD+Veh (Fig. 2F), in Kupffer cells in MD+PE (Fig. 4E), hub gene of the MD+PE WGCNA module (fig. S6D-E); Cyp4a12a up (CD-specific, fig. S2A-B); Acox1 used only as an in-vitro agonist control. Cpt1a: not reported.
- **Cholesterol synthesis:** mixed/down-leaning. Hmgcr down with PE under CD (fig. S2A-B); Tm7sf2, Ldlr, Mylip listed as MD+PE "cholesterol production" DEGs (Fig. 2E) but directions are not stated in the text.
- **Inflammation:** *up* spatially and histologically but modest in bulk. C3 up in CD+PE, MD+Veh and MD+PE vs CD+Veh (fig. S5A); inflammation-high spatial niches defined by a 9-gene signature; Nkap named as an inflammation DEG (direction not stated); lobular inflammation score only "an increasing trend" (Fig. 1H). No Tnf, Il1b, Nlrp3 or NF-kB result is reported.
- **Oxidative stress:** not reported (no ROS, MDA, SOD, GSH, Nrf2 or Hmox1 measurement or claim anywhere).
- **Mitochondria:** only mt-Nd6 up among shared PE DEGs (Fig. 2D); no functional mitochondrial assay.
- **Fibrosis:** explicitly absent - "Anxa2 expression in hepatocytes was significantly elevated following MD + PE treatment, despite the absence of overt fibrosis" (Discussion). No Sirius red/collagen staining reported.
- **Circadian:** not reported (no clock gene, no time-of-sacrifice statement anywhere in Methods).
- **MP x diet interaction:** claimed as "exacerbates diet-induced steatohepatitis" and "a potential synergistic effect of MD and PE" (Results), but **never formally tested as an interaction term**: all analyses are pairwise contrasts (one-way ANOVA + Tukey for phenotypes; pairwise DESeq2 contrasts for DEGs; "MD-specific PE genes" are defined by set subtraction of CD-condition DEGs, not by an interaction test).

## Author-acknowledged limitations
All are in one Discussion paragraph ("While this study provides high-resolution insights ... several limitations warrant consideration"):
1. Segmentation: "rigorous cellular segmentation in liver tissue remains technically challenging due to the complex, interdigitated morphology of hepatocytes and sinusoidal nonparenchymal cells. Although we used advanced algorithmic methods to define cellular boundaries, the possibility of minor signal spillover between adjacent cells cannot be entirely excluded."
2. Targeted panel: "the 10X Genomics Xenium platform uses a targeted high-resolution spatial transcriptomics approach ... this targeted strategy does not capture the full transcriptome. Consequently, the unbiased discovery of low-abundance transcripts or novel regulatory elements outside this predefined panel was beyond the scope".
3. Scope of the mechanism: "while our data strongly suggest the involvement of the PPARalpha-ANXA2 axis, this study primarily focuses on the mechanistic characterization of this pathway rather than evaluating PPARalpha modulation as a direct clinical therapeutic intervention."
Also stated, though framed as a finding rather than a limitation: "the proportion of central hepatocytes remained largely unchanged, which was unexpected" (Discussion), and the call for further work: "Further studies are needed to identify the upstream cellular pathways that lead to PPARalpha activation in this context".
Not acknowledged anywhere: male-only design, single dose, cage/group confounding, absence of an interaction test, absence of a non-plastic particle control, lack of particle characterisation or endotoxin testing, and the use of external datasets for the Ppara-null evidence.
Disclosure of AI use (Methods, Statistical analysis): "ChatGPT (version 4o and 5, OpenAI) and Gemini (version 3 Flash, Google) were used as language tools to refine our writing".

## Methodological points to verify independently
(Questions for the team, not accusations.)
1. **n per group for the bulk RNA-seq:** the paper never states it; Fig. 1B says n = 5 per group while Methods says "n = 4 or 5 per group". Does GEO GSE294180 have 4 or 5 CD+Veh libraries, and does the Fig. 1B legend overstate the phenotype n?
2. **Are the 19 Xenium samples the same 19 mice as the bulk libraries?** Methods says only "19 samples from two Xenium slides" and "matched mouse samples"; confirm the mapping before treating Fig. 4A as a within-animal validation.
3. **Sections per mouse and slide allocation:** were the two Xenium slides balanced across the four groups, or is slide confounded with group? Harmony was applied "across experimental groups and samples", which would also absorb genuine group differences - check what covariates Harmony was given.
4. **Unit of analysis in the spatial DE:** FindMarkers and the Anxa2 Spearman correlations appear to be cell-level with thousands of cells per mouse; only the diversity/proportion analyses model sample as a random effect. Which Fig. 4-6 p-values are mouse-level?
5. **Interaction:** no diet x PE term anywhere; "MD-specific PE genes" come from set subtraction, which is sensitive to power differences between the CD and MD contrasts (MD contrasts yielded more DEGs). Would a formal interaction model reproduce the gene lists?
6. **Batch/depth:** target was 15 M reads/sample; achieved depth, RIN, lane and library-prep batch are not reported. Was everything sequenced in one run?
7. **Particle characterisation:** no measured size distribution, no zeta potential, no endotoxin assay; PE is 10-150 um, i.e. mostly too large for cellular uptake - what is the assumed route to the liver?
8. **In-vitro screen comparability:** the seven polymers differ in nominal size by two orders of magnitude (55 um to 5000 um) at a fixed 100 ug/ml, so particle number and surface area differ greatly between arms; check whether the PE-is-most-toxic ranking survives surface-area normalisation, and whether table S1 reports replicate n.
9. **Raman/O-PTIR controls:** detection was only reported for MD+PE vs MD+Veh, on slides already processed for Xenium; no procedural/air blanks, no CD-group tissue, no quantification per gram, no blinding. How many particles in how many mice?
10. **Dose and units:** 2 mg/mouse/day is not body-weight normalised; the siRNA paragraph says "PE (100 uM)" where other text says 100 ug/ml - a probable typo to confirm.
11. **Ppara dependence:** Ppara-null evidence comes from GSE8295 (129S1/SvImJ, WY14643), not from PE-exposed mice; the siRNA/antagonist work is in primary hepatocytes. Knockdown efficiency is not shown. Is the in vivo PE-Ppara-Anxa2 chain established?
12. **ChIP-seq provenance:** SRX5142528 and SRX5028180 come from a GPS2-knockout study (ref. 58) - verify genotype, treatment and tissue of those two runs before citing them as wild-type liver PPARalpha binding; no peak caller or input control is named.
13. **Unreported covariates:** time of day at sacrifice, fasting state, food intake data (measured weekly but not shown), cage identity, body-weight covariate for liver-weight ratios.
14. **Multiple testing:** DESeq2 FDR is used for DEGs, but the WGCNA module-trait p-values and the GSEA with minimum gene-set size of 1 are not stated to be corrected across modules/traits/sets.
15. **Sex:** male only; no female data, so no sex-interaction claim can be checked.

## Relevance to the team
This is the source paper for GSE294180: it defines the 2 x 2 PE x MASH-diet design, the 8-week 2 mg/day oral gavage of 10-150 um Cospheric PE, the DESeq2 thresholds (|log2FC| >= 0.38, FDR < 0.05) and the pairwise-contrast strategy the team is re-examining. Its own "exacerbation"/"synergy" claims rest on one-way ANOVA and pairwise DESeq2 contrasts with no interaction term, and the Methods report neither per-group RNA-seq n, sequencing batch, achieved depth, time of sacrifice, nor cage as a unit - exactly the technical factors the team found to be collinear with PE. The mechanistic Ppara-Anxa2 conclusion is anchored in Xenium (a ~479-gene targeted panel, Zenodo not GEO), in vitro pharmacology/siRNA, ChIP-qPCR, and two external GEO datasets rather than in the bulk data themselves.
